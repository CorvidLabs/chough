# CLI requirements

These IDs describe durable behavior. Passing observations live separately in
`.chough/evidence/`, tied to repository content and environment. FRD provenance
and scope live in [docs/FRD.md](../../docs/FRD.md).

### REQ-cli-001 — Preserve user files

The helper SHALL initialize a resumable session and discoverable workflow skill
without replacing existing files. Trace: LIFE-4, LIFE-6.

Acceptance: repeated init preserves edited session and skill bytes; missing bundle
files can be added without overwriting neighbors.

### REQ-cli-002

Focus context for resumption. The helper SHALL assemble applicable instructions, scoped HI export, selected module contracts and companions, objective, decisions, open questions and next action. The native module lesson source SHALL be `<module>.3md` where present and otherwise the Lessons section of the module `context.md`; the helper SHALL point to it and SHALL NOT keep a lesson store, index or ledger of its own. The shipped skill SHALL tell agents to record each lesson in that source with a certainty label (observation, hypothesis or verified), its source and its limits, and SHALL state that a project decision always outranks a lesson. The shipped skill SHALL ask an agent resuming work to state in its own message, before its first edit, the goal, each recorded decision that binds the next task with its reason, the last failure with its bug and status, the lessons that apply and the next task, and to say what the records do not tell it instead of guessing. Trace: LIFE-3, LIFE-6, LIFE-1, FLOW-12, FLOW-13.

Acceptance Criteria
- The same inputs yield the same ordered context; missing sources and truncation remain explicit.
- Native module .3md is the lesson source where present; otherwise the module context.md is used.
- The canonical skill states the three certainty labels, that each lesson names its source and limits, and that a project decision outranks a lesson; no Chough lesson store, index or ledger exists.
- The canonical skill's resume steps ask the agent to state the goal, binding decisions with reasons, the last failure with its bug status, the applicable lessons and the next task in its own message before its first edit, and to name what the records do not say.

### REQ-cli-003

The helper SHALL report actual native SpecSync status and guidance, with unavailable, failed, undecodable, oversized or timed-out responses explicitly unknown. A failed native command SHALL be shown with its result in plain words, `exit <code>` or `killed by signal <number>`, never a program's internal debug form. Native reads SHALL retain at most the parse bound of standard output and a display bound of standard error while reading, and SHALL end by a bounded deadline. Trace: LIFE-1, LIFE-6, LIFE-7.

Acceptance Criteria
- Missing SpecSync never becomes an approved or delivered state, and an agent-written session next action never grants approval or overrides a native gate.
- A native command that never exits is reported as timed-out by its deadline, and gated moves refuse it.
- Output beyond the bounds is counted and reported, not retained.
- A native command that exits 2 is shown as `exit 2`, and one ended by SIGTERM as `killed by signal 15`.

### REQ-cli-004

Bind observations to content and environment. The helper SHALL record with each observation the Git revision, a working content identity and an explicit environment, and SHALL report freshness limitations. Freshness SHALL compare the working content identity, which covers every tracked or untracked non-ignored file except Chough's own records, build output, the generated knowledge directory (the librarian's configured library dir) and SpecSync lifecycle bookkeeping (the state, approval, verification, review, finalization, accepted-state and generated lesson-bundle records of a change and the lifecycle state field of its front matter); a change's deltas and other artifacts remain covered wherever the change directory is. The recorded revision SHALL be kept for display and regression ancestry and SHALL NOT by itself make an observation stale. An observation recorded without a content identity SHALL keep the revision-bound comparison. Trace: LIFE-2, LIFE-6, LIFE-7.

Acceptance Criteria
- Changing observed content, including a native change's delta or artifacts before materialization, makes prior observations stale; another environment is mismatched; no chosen environment is unchecked.
- Recording another observation, editing the session, committing only Chough records, an empty commit, SpecSync lifecycle bookkeeping writes and archiving a change do not self-invalidate the content identity.
- An observation without a content identity remains stale after any new commit.
- Rebuilding the knowledge index or vault after recording an observation does not make that observation stale.

Acceptance Criteria
- An optional retained snapshot.input_inventory is diagnostic metadata only. Reading valid supported inventory SHALL preserve its stored completeness and SHALL NOT change aggregate content/fingerprint comparisons, artifact/case/environment/ancestry rules, historical outcomes or authority. The reader SHALL NOT infer changed paths or completeness from absent, partial or unsupported inventory. New observations SHALL retain existing writer behavior; this reader change SHALL NOT generate inventories.

### REQ-cli-005

Validate traceability. The helper SHALL validate observation criterion IDs, requirement IDs and contract paths against their native records before recording. A requirement that the active session's native change adds or modifies in its deltas SHALL count as resolved, as it does for a case run, so evidence can be recorded before `specsync change check` materializes it. `chough evidence add` SHALL require at least one `--criterion`, and `--requirement` and `--contract` SHALL be optional, each validated when given. A refused requirement link SHALL name the companion files and the native change's deltas searched and a likely cause with its fix: the module whose companion holds the ID when it belongs to another module, otherwise a typo. The helper SHALL NOT accept an unresolved link. Trace: LIFE-1, LIFE-2, LIFE-5, FLOW-7.

Acceptance Criteria
- Unknown active HI IDs or absent native requirement IDs are refused; observation IDs cannot overwrite prior observations.
- Observations are unauthenticated claims, including those labelled human or delivery observations.
- A requirement of another module is refused with that module and the fix named; a typo and an unmaterialized delta ID are refused with the check-first hint.
- With a requirement that only the active session's native change adds in its deltas, `chough evidence add --requirement <it>` records; a requirement in neither the companions nor the deltas is refused naming both.
- `chough evidence add` with `--criterion` and no `--requirement` or `--contract` records; without `--criterion` it is refused.

### REQ-cli-006

The helper SHALL bound assembled context and reject unsupported records or paths escaping the project. Trace: LIFE-3, LIFE-4.

Acceptance: traversal, invalid module/record IDs and symlink escape attempts fail; large context is visibly bounded. Local artifacts remain reviewable plain files.

Acceptance Criteria
- The reader SHALL accept optional diagnostic inventory v1 with strict inventory fields version:u32, complete:bool, entries and strict entry fields domain:u8, key, path, digest. Inventory SHALL contain at most128 entries and at most32768 compact serialized JSON bytes. v1 entries SHALL have unique safe nonempty relative normal paths, valid SHA256 digests and domain/key matching the existing canonical fingerprint_key classification. Unknown fields/types, invalid semantic entries, exceeded limits and unsupported versions SHALL refuse explicitly without mutating observations. Absent/null inventory SHALL preserve existing legacy reading behavior and existing whole-record bounds.

### REQ-cli-007

Canonical checkpoint. The helper SHALL keep one canonical checkpoint per open session: the front matter of the active session's `session.md` (REQ-records-004, REQ-cli-037), holding the objective, intent scope, modules, current stage with its status, iteration and side-stage callers, the native change identity, the pull request, referenced criteria, requirements, cases, design sources and files, observation and bug references, blockers, awaiting-human gates, run consents and the next action, with decisions, completed work and open questions in the story sections of the same file. No other writable checkpoint store SHALL exist and no adapter such as APS SHALL be required. Every read SHALL validate the front matter's keys and value types and SHALL refuse a malformed one naming the file, the key and the rule (REQ-records-001). `chough checkpoint --merge` and the `chough-session` merge driver are removed, because each session has its own folder and parallel branches never write one checkpoint (DRIVE-24). When SpecSync is configured, `chough checkpoint --native-change <id>` SHALL read `specsync change status <id>` before it applies any update, and SHALL refuse as `refused:native-change-unknown`, naming the ID and that status command, when SpecSync does not report the change; a failed or timed-out read SHALL also refuse, and a reply whose `summary.id` or `change.id` names a different change SHALL refuse as `refused:native-change-mismatch`, naming both IDs. A JSON reply that names neither `summary.id` nor `change.id` SHALL refuse as `refused:native-change-unknown`, since it does not identify the requested change. Every such refusal SHALL leave the session file byte-identical, including the other updates given in the same call. An ID SpecSync reports, archived or open, SHALL be saved as before. Without a SpecSync configuration only the existing ID format check SHALL apply, with no native read. Trace: FLOW-13, LIFE-3, DRIVE-3, DRIVE-24, DRIVE-25.

Acceptance Criteria
- A checkpoint saved by one process is read unchanged by `chough context` and `chough next` in another process; a front matter key with the wrong type is refused naming the file and the key, with nothing written.
- No second writable checkpoint store is introduced and no adapter is required; `.gitattributes` declares no `chough-session` driver and `chough checkpoint --merge` is not a command.
- With SpecSync configured, `checkpoint --native-change` for an ID SpecSync does not report, or after a failed or timed-out status read, exits non-zero with `refused:native-change-unknown` or the read failure and leaves the session file byte-identical even with other updates in the same call; a reply naming a different change refuses as `refused:native-change-mismatch` with the file byte-identical; a JSON reply naming no change ID, such as `[]` or `{}`, refuses as `refused:native-change-unknown` with the file byte-identical; a reported ID saves; without SpecSync any well-formed ID saves and a malformed one is refused.

### REQ-cli-008

Stage routing bounded by native gates. The helper SHALL route the stages intent, design, plan, build, test, accept and done, the optional release stage, and the side stages bugs, validate and librarian. It SHALL record each advance, iterate, back, reset, pause, resume, skip and side-stage enter or return as an append-only transition with its reason. Back and reset SHALL list the later stages whose outputs need refresh and SHALL point to native SpecSync status for any reapproval or recovery. Every move SHALL meet the prerequisite of its destination, whichever command makes it: build SHALL require the recorded native change to be approved, implementing or verifying with a valid definition approval; test and accept SHALL require the build prerequisite or the done prerequisite; done SHALL require the change to be archived with authenticated terminal evidence. Unavailable, unknown or undecodable native state, an approval reported invalid, unauthenticated archive evidence and an invalid SDD policy SHALL refuse those moves; a valid disabled policy or no policy SHALL leave them ungated. The helper SHALL NOT record or infer human approval or review. Each `chough stage` move SHALL print by default a short text summary of what it applied, the move, the stage it reached and the next action, one line each, and SHALL print its full JSON report, with every field it gave before, under `--json`, so an agent loses nothing. Leaving test for accept, by advance or skip, SHALL also be refused with `refused:unlinked-failure` while the latest executed (passed or failed) observation, by recorded time and then ID, of a case referenced in the checkpoint failed and no local bug links that observation; a later `not-run` observation executed nothing and SHALL NOT replace it; the refusal SHALL name the case and the observation and SHALL say to rerun the case or link the observation to a bug. A later passed observation of that case, or any local bug linking the failed observation, SHALL lift it; stale passed observations, cases with only `not-run` observations and cases not referenced in the checkpoint SHALL NOT trigger it. At test, `chough next` and `chough status` SHALL name the same refusal from the same rule. Trace: FLOW-2, FLOW-5, FLOW-10, LIFE-1, FLOW-7, LIFE-5.

Acceptance Criteria
- Every transition kind is exercised in an isolated fixture; history survives back and reset; skip without a reason is refused.
- Entering build is refused for draft, unavailable and approval-invalid states and allowed for a valid approved state.
- Reaching test or accept is refused for draft, unavailable and approval-invalid states and allowed for a valid approved state or an archive with authenticated terminal evidence.
- Leaving accept, reset to done and release to done are refused unless the change is archived with authenticated terminal evidence.
- A malformed or ill-typed SDD policy refuses gated moves; a valid disabled policy leaves them ungated.
- Entering a side stage records its caller, and return restores that caller.
- Leaving test with a referenced case whose latest executed (passed or failed) observation failed and that no bug links is refused with `refused:unlinked-failure` naming the case, the observation and both remedies, for advance and skip, even when a later observation is only `not-run`; a later pass or a linking bug of any status allows it; a stale passed observation, a not-run-only case or an unreferenced failed case does not refuse; at test, next and status name the same refusal.
- `chough stage advance` prints at most 10 lines of text naming the move, the stage reached and the next action; `chough stage advance --json` prints the full JSON report.

### REQ-cli-009

Validated test definitions. The helper SHALL validate test definitions in `.chough/cases/<module>.cases.toml` against profile `chough-testing/1`: unique stable case IDs, active HI criteria, native requirement IDs, track, method, environments, artifact types, explicit expected outcomes, setup and steps for manual or agent-assisted cases, and a binding for automated cases. A binding MAY declare `env`, a bounded table of environment variable names and values; for the `cargo-test` adapter it SHALL NOT declare a variable that chooses what cargo builds or runs, and no binding SHALL declare an expected-output fragment containing the escape character. Unknown fields and unknown profiles SHALL be rejected. Each case SHALL have a definition digest that is independent of its position in the file, and an omitted or empty `env` SHALL leave that digest unchanged. New case definitions SHALL use curated IDs in the form `TC-<module>-<title-slug>`, where the title slug consists of nonempty lowercase ASCII alphanumeric runs separated by single hyphens. The complete ID SHALL remain within the existing 100-byte record ID bound using a module-aware slug limit. Case IDs SHALL be globally unique across all modules, including legacy and new forms. Exact legacy `TC-<module>-NNNN` definitions SHALL remain valid with unchanged definition digests and without renumbering or migration. New regression case definitions introduced for this change SHALL use slug IDs. Documentation SHALL state that older Chough versions require an upgrade to use slug case IDs. A requirement ID the companion lacks SHALL be accepted only when the checkpoint's native change, on one native read per command, passes the build gate checks of REQ-cli-022 and its delta for the case module adds or modifies that ID; otherwise it SHALL be refused, naming why a consulted delta did not resolve it. The shipped skill SHALL say so, SHALL show the test stage linking failing-first evidence, and SHALL tell agents not to list running or recording cases as a `tasks.md` checkbox. Trace: FLOW-7, LIFE-2, LIFE-6, DRIVE-6.

Acceptance Criteria
- Reordering cases leaves their IDs and digests unchanged; editing expected outcomes, steps or a binding, including its `env`, changes the digest.
- Duplicate IDs, unknown criteria or requirements, unknown fields, missing expected outcomes and automated cases without a binding are refused with the offending location.
- An invalid `env` name, a name over 128 bytes, two names equal ignoring ASCII case, a value with NUL, more than 32 entries or a value over 4 KiB, and an expected-output fragment containing ESC are refused with the offending location.
- A `cargo-test` binding that declares PATH, CARGO, CARGO_HOME, RUSTC, RUSTDOC, RUSTC_WRAPPER, RUSTC_WORKSPACE_WRAPPER, RUSTFLAGS, CARGO_ENCODED_RUSTFLAGS, RUSTDOCFLAGS, CARGO_ENCODED_RUSTDOCFLAGS, RUSTC_BOOTSTRAP, or a name starting with RUSTUP_, CARGO_BUILD_, CARGO_TARGET_ (other than CARGO_TARGET_DIR), CARGO_HOST_, CARGO_PROFILE_ or CARGO_UNSTABLE_, in any ASCII case, is refused with the offending location.
- Curated slug IDs accept lowercase ASCII normalization and enforce the module-aware 100-byte bound; empty, uppercase, repeated-separator and leading or trailing separator forms are refused. A duplicate ID in another module is refused globally.
- Exact legacy numeric cases remain readable and executable with unchanged definition digests and no renumbering or migration. The new regression case IDs for this change use slugs rather than allocating legacy numbers.
- A case linking a requirement only the approved, unchanged delta adds passes `cases check`, `run` and `record` on one native read and is listed under `approved_delta_requirements`; an edited delta, invalid approval, wrong state or module, no change or a failed read refuses with the reason.
- The plan, testing and accept references describe this and failing-first evidence in the test stage.

### REQ-cli-010

Focused case selection. The helper SHALL select active cases linked to the given affected criteria, requirements or paths, always including their functional cases, adding copy-localization and design-QA cases only when those tracks are requested as relevant, and SHALL report every omitted case with its reason together with any configured project-required lane. Trace: FLOW-6.

Acceptance Criteria
- An affected requirement selects its linked functional cases and omits unrelated cases with a reason.
- Design-QA and copy-localization cases are selected only when their track is requested.

### REQ-cli-011

Honest case execution. When explicitly invoked, the helper SHALL execute an automated case's binding directly without a shell, with the binding's declared environment set on top of the inherited one, capture its output to the local log of REQ-records-005, and record an evidence entry (REQ-cli-017) linked to the case and its definition digest. Execution SHALL end by the binding's deadline: on the deadline, or when output stays open after the command exits, the helper SHALL stop the command and its descendants and SHALL NOT wait on output it cannot complete. On Unix, when the helper is stopped by SIGINT, SIGTERM or SIGHUP while it runs a binding, a native read or one of its own Git commands, or while it saves what a binding reported, it SHALL stop every command it started together with that command's process group and, on Linux, every descendant that left that group (for example with `setsid`), including one orphaned by its parent, record nothing partial and end by that signal: a stop before an automated case's save begins SHALL record no evidence entry, log or checkpoint change, and a stop that arrives once the save has begun SHALL let the log and the entry both be written before the helper ends by that signal. A signal inherited as ignored, or as blocked in the signal mask, SHALL keep its inherited disposition. When a descendant of one of the helper's own Git commands keeps that command's output open past the grace period and is stopped, the helper SHALL say so on stderr; when its `git rev-parse HEAD` fails for a reason other than an absent repository or a HEAD with no commit, it SHALL say so on stderr and record no revision. Captured output SHALL be bounded per stream while reading. The log SHALL keep the captured bytes, and the entry their SHA-256 and key failing lines (REQ-records-005), while assertions SHALL be evaluated on that text with complete terminal escape sequences removed. Removal SHALL never remove a line break, and a sequence that does not complete before a line break or the end of the output SHALL be left unchanged. The observation SHALL be passed only when the process exits zero, its capture is complete and the binding's assertion holds: exactly one test ran for the `cargo-test` adapter, or every declared expected-output fragment appeared for the `command` and `fledge-task` adapters. Every observation of an executed case SHALL state as a limitation that inherited environment variables were not recorded; when the binding declares variables it SHALL also name them without their values, and when escape sequences were removed it SHALL say so. A run in which no test matched SHALL be recorded as not-run. A repeated run SHALL record one evidence entry holding each attempt and a summary with attempt and failure counts and elapsed time, and SHALL start no further attempt once a stop request or a paused checkpoint exists, including after waiting its interval. Automatic observation and repeated validation run allocation SHALL follow REQ-cli-023. An explicit evidence ID SHALL remain optional for automated and manual case recording; when omitted, the helper SHALL name the entry by REQ-records-003, and when supplied for a new write it SHALL be a date and slug name not in use. An old-style supplied ID SHALL be refused with a remedy to omit the ID. The automated log SHALL be exactly `.chough/local/logs/<evidence id>.log`, inheriting the entry's name, and SHALL NOT be committed. Observation and validation run IDs from 0.5.x SHALL stay readable and resumable as aliases. A successful `cases record` response SHALL retain every existing key and SHALL add `case` and `observation`, containing the recorded case ID and observation ID respectively; the observation ID SHALL equal both the evidence file stem in `recorded` and the stored record's `id`. Trace: FLOW-7, FLOW-9, LIFE-7, FLOW-14, LIFE-19, KNOW-35.

Acceptance Criteria
- A deliberately failing assertion records failed even when another case passes in the same session.
- A cargo filter that matches no test records not-run, never passed.
- Changing a case definition reports its earlier observations as stale-case-definition.
- A case whose descendant holds its output returns by its deadline, and its descendants are stopped.
- SIGINT, SIGTERM or SIGHUP during `cases run` before its save begins, during a native read in `next`, or during the Git that `stage backfill` or `library snapshot` runs before writing leaves no process Chough started, Git included, and no new or changed `.chough` or library file; a stop once a case run's save has begun leaves both its log and its evidence entry, and no process Chough started.
- A stop request made during the interval halts a repeated run before the next attempt, and resuming continues with the stored limits.
- A declared variable reaches the command, and the observation names it without its value.
- An observation of a case that declares no variables still states that inherited environment variables were not recorded.
- An escape sequence interrupted by a line break or the end of the output is left unchanged, and removal never lowers the number of lines that start with `test result:`.
- Coloured output whose visible text holds a fragment passes with the escape limitation, the log stays byte-exact, and a fragment absent from the visible text still fails.
- Omitting the evidence ID names the entry `<date>-<case slug>`, identical repeated runs on one day receive distinct names, and each automated log is exactly `.chough/local/logs/<evidence id>.log`, ignored by Git.
- Repeated validation records one entry named `<date>-<case slug>-run` with every attempt. Migrated observation and validation IDs remain readable and resumable as aliases, while an old-style ID supplied for a new case observation fails with an omit-ID remedy.
- On Linux, SIGTERM during `cases run` also stops a descendant that called `setsid`, and one whose parent exited after starting it with `setsid`.
- A Git descendant that holds output past the grace period is stopped with a stderr notice, and a failed `git rev-parse HEAD` is reported on stderr while the transition is still recorded.
- A successful `cases record` response returns `case` and `observation` beside every pre-existing key; `observation` equals the `recorded` path's file stem and the stored record's `id`.

### REQ-cli-012

Local bug backlog. The helper SHALL keep each bug as one Markdown record, `.chough/bugs/<id>.md` (REQ-records-001), whose front matter gives `kind: bug`, `id`, `aliases`, `title`, `status`, `classification`, `scope`, `impact`, `criteria`, `requirements`, `cases`, `evidence` (the linked evidence IDs), `receipts` (the receipt fields of REQ-history-006 for linked evidence no longer in an open session), `regression_baseline`, `fixture`, `environment`, `revision`, `found_by` (the actor, REQ-records-007), `fixed_in`, `duplicate_of`, `related`, `created` and `updated`, where `regression_baseline`, `fixture`, `fixed_in` and `duplicate_of` are written only when set and a missing one reads as empty, and whose body gives `# <title>` and the paragraphs What happened (the symptom and actual behavior), Steps (the trigger, labelled `Trigger:`, then each step), Expected, Why it matters and Fix (`Not fixed yet.` until `chough bug update --fix <text>` sets it, each set also logged; marking a bug verified while its Fix still says `Not fixed yet.` SHALL replace that with a line naming the passing observation and its revision), then `## Log`, an append-only list with one dated entry per report, note, evidence link and status change. It SHALL list open bugs for a fresh session and SHALL search existing records by symptom, trigger and affected IDs before a new record is added. A bug SHALL remain unverified until a failed observation is linked, and a regression classification SHALL require a passed observation at an ancestor revision. A bug SHALL read as reproduced and later passed, and SHALL accept the verified status, only when a present linked passed observation was observed strictly after its most recent present linked failed observation; a receipt counts as present, and an observation shown as removed (REQ-records-009) does not. Additional evidence SHALL be appended to the Log, never overwriting anything. `chough bug add` and `chough bug update` given `--observation` with an evidence entry of the active session SHALL also add the bug to that session's checkpoint (`checkpoint --bug`, REQ-records-004), so the session's story names the bugs found in it. A bug in another tool's behaviour MAY be closed as `fixed-upstream` with `bug update --status fixed-upstream --note <text>`, the note naming the upstream fix and who checked it where; it is a closed status, as `verified`, `duplicate` and `wont-fix` are, needs no Chough observation, and SHALL never read as verified by Chough. The helper SHALL render a report candidate and SHALL NOT publish it. Every new bug SHALL be named by REQ-records-003 from its title, the same for manual `bug add` and automatic failure capture, and written by create-new; an update SHALL NOT rename the record, even when its title has changed. Every `BUG-NNNN` and `BUG-<slug>-<hex>` ID from 0.5.x SHALL resolve as an alias, and duplicate and related links SHALL resolve in either direction across aliases and new names. Documentation SHALL state that Chough 0.5.x cannot read 0.6.0 bug records. Trace: FLOW-8, LIFE-5, LIFE-8, DRIVE-25, DRIVE-27.

Acceptance Criteria
- A sample failure becomes a reproduced and classified bug; a second report of the same symptom is found by search and its evidence is appended to the Log.
- A regression classification without a passed observation at an ancestor revision is refused.
- An out-of-scope bug stays in the open backlog without changing the checkpoint scope.
- Rendering a report candidate prints Markdown and performs no network action.
- A pass observed before, or in the same second as, the latest linked failure leaves the bug reproduced and the verified status is refused; a bug with no linked failure cannot be marked verified.
- `--classification blocking` is refused with a hint to use `--scope`, and `--scope product-defect` with a hint to use `--classification`.
- Manual `bug add` and automatic failure capture both name the bug `<date>-<title slug>`, and changing a stored title and then updating its status or evidence leaves the name unchanged.
- Each update appends one Log entry and leaves every earlier Log line, and every body paragraph a person wrote, byte-identical.
- A migrated bug answers to its `BUG-NNNN` alias in list, search, show, update and report, and duplicate and related links resolve from alias to new name and back.
- After the session holding a bug's linked evidence is archived, the bug resolves that evidence to the story's receipt and its verified state is unchanged.
- The CLI documentation says Chough 0.5.x cannot read 0.6.0 bug records.
- `bug update --status fixed-upstream` without a note is refused; with one it closes the bug, `bug list` shows it closed as fixed upstream, and nothing reports it verified.
- A new bug's Steps start with `Trigger:` and the trigger; `bug update --fix <text>` sets the Fix paragraph and logs it; a bug marked verified with no Fix set says which observation passed at which revision instead of `Not fixed yet.`
- A bug added, or updated, with `--observation` naming an evidence entry of the active session is in that session's checkpoint and in its archived story; one naming another session's entry leaves the active checkpoint unchanged.

### REQ-cli-013

Preferences and run consent. The helper SHALL keep standing project preferences in `.chough/preferences.json`, each with value, source and scope, separately from run consents stored in the checkpoint for the current objective. Repeated validation, monitoring, release routing and monitor initiation SHALL default to ask, ask, on-request and capture-only respectively; the librarian SHALL be on by default, as REQ-cli-015 states, and off only by a recorded preference. A run consent SHALL never be written as a standing preference, and changing the objective SHALL clear run consents. Trace: FLOW-9, FLOW-11, FLOW-12.

Acceptance Criteria
- Granting a run consent leaves `.chough/preferences.json` byte-identical; setting a standing preference requires a source.
- Repeated validation is refused without a consent or standing preference, and a new objective clears the consent.
- Failures found by repeated validation create local bug records that remain discoverable after restart; monitor initiation proposes a native change command only when configured and otherwise captures locally.
- With no preference recorded, `chough pref show` reports the librarian `on` with origin `default`, and no requirement gives it another default.

### REQ-cli-014

Optional release routing. The helper SHALL advance from accept to done and never to release, and SHALL allow entering release only with a release run consent or a standing release preference. Release observations SHALL use distinct kinds for publication, installation, deployment, live health and dry runs, and a dry run SHALL never satisfy a delivery kind. Trace: FLOW-11, LIFE-7.

Acceptance Criteria
- Advancing from accept never enters release, and entering release without authorization is refused.
- A dry-run observation is reported as a dry run, not as deployment or live health.
- Publication, installation, deployment and live-health observations are each accepted and reported with their own kind, and each is kept with its kind as a receipt of its archived story; a dry run's receipt still reads as a dry run.

### REQ-cli-015

Project librarian. The librarian SHALL be on by default in `knowledge/` (or `library-dir`) and off only by a recorded preference. It SHALL keep the vault of REQ-cli-044 and SHALL never move, copy, rewrite or replace a canonical source or a Chough record (KNOW-30): it regenerates `knowledge/index.md` and the journal feed deterministically from their sources (REQ-cli-043, REQ-records-011). `chough library snapshot` and `chough library archive` are removed, since a snapshot copies a source and the archive step trimmed proof; existing `library/` snapshots and `briefs/` SHALL stay byte-identical, readable and linked from the index as they are (KNOW-6). No gate SHALL read the vault. It SHALL be usable as a side stage from any stage. Trace: FLOW-12, KNOW-1, KNOW-3, KNOW-5, KNOW-6, KNOW-12, KNOW-30, KNOW-31.

Acceptance Criteria
- Checkpoints and resume work with the librarian off; with no preference it is on in `knowledge/`.
- Existing snapshots and briefs stay byte-identical and are linked from `knowledge/index.md`.
- Regenerating gives identical bytes, and no step of a fixture cycle writes a copy of a record or source under `knowledge/`.
- `chough library snapshot` and `chough library archive` are not commands, and the help names `library index` instead.

### REQ-cli-016

Host skill installation. The helper SHALL install the complete skill bundle once for each distinct supported host directory requested, defaulting to `.agents/skills`, with the claude host sharing `.agents/skills`; SHALL report each installed file as current, edited, known-outdated or missing; SHALL update only missing files and files byte-identical to a known earlier bundle; SHALL report an installed legacy `.claude/skills/chough-workflow/` copy as legacy without writing or deleting it during init, status or update; and SHALL remove legacy files only through an explicit remove-legacy command that requires the shared copy, removes only files byte-identical to the current or a known earlier bundle and keeps every other file.
