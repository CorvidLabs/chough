# Chough 0.6.0 human-first records: the record format, names, local facts, identity, migration and journal

## ADDED

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-001

Record files. Every record Chough writes, a session, an archived session story, an evidence entry, a bug, an inbox entry, a journal post and a migration receipt, SHALL be one UTF-8 Markdown file that starts with YAML front matter between two `---` lines, and that file SHALL be the record's only source of truth: no JSON or other twin SHALL be written beside it, and no index, cache or generated note SHALL be read in its place. The front matter SHALL carry the IDs and fields agents parse; the body SHALL carry the record for a human reader. Front matter SHALL use a strict subset of YAML that Chough reads and writes without a new dependency: `key: value` scalars, plain or double-quoted with `\"`, `\\` and `\n` escapes; flow lists `[a, b]`; block lists of scalars; and block lists of flat maps of scalars. Chough SHALL refuse front matter outside that subset, a duplicate key or a missing closing `---`, naming the file, the line and the rule, and SHALL write nothing in that command. Reading SHALL be tolerant: an unknown front matter key SHALL be kept byte for byte, in place, when Chough rewrites the front matter, and SHALL be ignored otherwise; a missing optional key SHALL take its documented default; CRLF line endings SHALL be read as LF. Chough SHALL own only the front matter and the sections it appends to, and SHALL keep every other body byte a person or agent wrote: a write SHALL replace the front matter, append list items to the end of a named section, or create a missing named section at the end of the body, and SHALL do nothing else to an existing body. Files SHALL be written with LF line endings by an atomic replace under the writer lock of REQ-cli-040, or by create-new for a new record. A record SHALL be at most 65,536 bytes, except a session record, an archived session story and a migration receipt, which SHALL be at most 1,048,576, 1,048,576 and 4,194,304 bytes; a record over its bound SHALL be refused with the bound named and every file byte-identical. Trace: DRIVE-25, DRIVE-26, DRIVE-28, KNOW-32, LIFE-3.

Acceptance Criteria
- Every record kind written by a fixture cycle (session, archived story, evidence, bug, inbox entry, journal post, migration receipt) starts with front matter that the reader parses, and no `.json` file is written under `.chough/` except the local caches of REQ-history-007 and `.chough/preferences.json`.
- A person's edit to a session's Goal paragraph, to a bug's Steps and to an evidence entry's body survives a following stage move, decision, evidence add and bug update byte for byte.
- An unknown front matter key added by hand is kept byte for byte after Chough rewrites that front matter, and a missing optional key reads as its default.
- Front matter with a nested map, an anchor, a duplicate key or no closing line is refused with the file, line and rule named, and nothing is written.
- A CRLF copy of a record reads the same as the LF original.
- A record one byte over its bound is refused naming the bound, with every file unchanged.

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-002

The records tree and its reading guides. Chough's committed records SHALL live in this tree: `.chough/README.md`; `.chough/sessions/<id>/session.md` with its `evidence/` and `agent/` folders, for open sessions; `.chough/archive/sessions/<id>.md`, one file per finished session, with `.chough/archive/sessions/<id>/` beside it only for its key screenshots (REQ-records-013); `.chough/archive/shelved/<id>/`, for shelved sessions; `.chough/archive/migrations/<id>.md`, for migration receipts; `.chough/bugs/<id>.md`; `.chough/cases/*.cases.toml`, case definitions in their existing TOML format (REQ-cli-009, REQ-cli-047); `.chough/preferences.json` (REQ-cli-013); and, kept from 0.5.x, the files under `.chough/artifacts/` that an open bug names as its fixture (REQ-records-009). Machine-local facts SHALL live only in the Git-ignored `.chough/local/` (REQ-history-007). Each kind of file SHALL have a stated rule: a session folder lives while its work is open and leaves the tree once its story is written and its bytes are committed; an archived story stays for good; a bug file stays for good; case definitions and preferences are configuration a person edits; and machine-only files live in `.chough/local/` and are never committed. `chough init` and `chough migrate --apply` SHALL write `.chough/README.md` create-only, a one-screen guide in plain words that names each folder, what it holds and its rule, which folder is never committed, and that intent lives in `hi/`, contracts in `specs/` and change history in `.specsync/archive/`, each a link, with no copy of their content; an existing `.chough/README.md` SHALL be kept byte-identical. `knowledge/index.md` is the vault's reading guide (REQ-cli-043). Chough SHALL write no other committed file or folder under `.chough/`, and SHALL NOT read a 0.5.x path except in `chough migrate` and `chough doctor` (REQ-records-010). Trace: DRIVE-24, DRIVE-28, KNOW-32, KNOW-24.

Acceptance Criteria
- After `chough init` in a new project, `.chough/` holds `README.md`, `cases/` and `local/` (ignored by Git), and `.chough/README.md` names every folder of the tree, says that `local/` is never committed, and links `hi/`, `specs/` and `.specsync/archive/`.
- `chough init` and `chough migrate --apply` over an existing `.chough/README.md` keep it byte-identical.
- A full fixture cycle, from session new through done with a bug, cases and evidence, leaves committed files under `.chough/` only at the paths this requirement lists.

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-003

Date and slug names. A new session, evidence entry, bug, inbox entry, journal post and migration receipt SHALL be named `<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>`, the date being the UTC date of its creation and the slug the lowercase ASCII letters and digits of its source (a session key, a case ID or claim, a bug title, a post title), each maximal run of other characters replaced by one hyphen and leading and trailing hyphens trimmed, with the record kind as the slug when nothing is left. The whole name SHALL be at most 80 bytes, the slug cut back at a hyphen when it would exceed that. A record SHALL never be named by a sequence number, a counter or a high-water mark. When a file of that name already exists in the same folder at creation, the new record SHALL get `-` and 6 lowercase hexadecimal digits from 24 fresh random bits (REQ-cli-023's random source), written by create-new; if that name also exists the write SHALL be refused, with nothing written, and SHALL NOT draw again. A record's name SHALL NOT change after creation. The record's front matter SHALL give its name as `id`. Every ID a record had before 0.6.0 (a `BUG-NNNN` or `BUG-<slug>-<hex>` bug, an `OBS-`, `VAL-` or `SNAP-` record, a session key) SHALL be kept in its front matter `aliases` list, and every command and link that takes a record ID SHALL resolve it by `id` or by alias; an alias held by two records SHALL be refused by the command that resolves it and reported by `chough doctor` (REQ-history-012). SpecSync requirement IDs, case IDs, lesson numbering and spec versions SHALL keep their schemes. Trace: DRIVE-27, DRIVE-12, DRIVE-25.

Acceptance Criteria
- `  Fix   LOGIN... now!  ` recorded as a bug on 2026-11-10 is named `2026-11-10-fix-login-now`, and a title with no letter or digit gives `2026-11-10-bug`; an overlong title gives a name of at most 80 bytes that ends without a hyphen.
- Two bugs with the same title on the same day get `2026-11-10-<slug>` and `2026-11-10-<slug>-<6 hex>`; a forced clash of the suffix too is refused with nothing written and no second draw.
- Two branches that each add a bug and an evidence entry on the same day with different titles merge with no conflict.
- A migrated bug with alias `BUG-0012` is shown, updated and linked by `BUG-0012` and by its new name, and a session referencing `OBS-<slug>-<hex>` resolves it to the migrated evidence; an alias given to two records is refused by `bug show` and reported by `chough doctor`.
- No new record's name holds a number taken from a count of existing records.

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-004

The session record. An open session's `session.md` front matter SHALL be its checkpoint and SHALL give `kind: session`, `id`, `aliases`, `objective`, `intent_scope`, `modules`, `stage`, `stage_status`, `iteration`, `callers`, `native_change`, `pr`, `criteria`, `requirements`, `cases`, `design`, `files`, `observations`, `bugs`, `blockers`, `waiting_on_human` (each gate with `gate`, `holds` and `opened`, REQ-cli-030), `run_consents` and `next`. Its body SHALL be the story, in these sections in this order: `# <title>` (the objective), `## Goal`, `## Where it stands`, `## Decisions and why`, `## What was verified`, `## Open threads` and `## Timeline` (REQ-history-002). A decision recorded with `chough checkpoint --decision` SHALL be one list item appended to Decisions and why, naming who decided when the decision is a human's (REQ-cli-050) and linking the gate's timeline entry; completed work SHALL be appended to Where it stands; an open question SHALL be one list item in Open threads, resolved by appending its answer to that item's line through `chough checkpoint --resolve-question`; an evidence entry referenced by the session SHALL be one list item in What was verified that links its file. These sections SHALL be the only place the session keeps decisions, completed work and open questions, and `chough next` and `chough status` SHALL read them there (REQ-cli-021), so no field repeats them. Chough SHALL write the Goal from the objective when the session is created and SHALL NOT rewrite the Goal or Where it stands paragraphs afterwards. Trace: DRIVE-24, DRIVE-25, DRIVE-26, FLOW-13.

Acceptance Criteria
- `chough session new` writes a `session.md` whose front matter carries every listed key and whose body has the seven parts in order with the Goal taken from the objective.
- A decision, a completed item, an open question and an evidence reference each appear once, in their section, and `chough next` shows the same decision and question from a fresh process.
- Resolving an open question keeps its line and appends the answer, and `chough next` no longer lists it as open.
- A person's rewrite of the Goal paragraph is kept after a decision and a stage move.

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-005

Proof files: logs, key failing lines and external evidence. A raw log SHALL never be committed. The raw output of an executed case SHALL be written to `.chough/local/logs/<evidence id>.log`, and its evidence entry SHALL give the exact command, the SHA-256 of the captured output as its fingerprint and, when a CI run holds the log, `raw_log` naming that run. For a failed or not-run case run, the entry's body SHALL also keep the key failing lines: at most 20 lines and 2,048 bytes of the captured output, with complete terminal escape sequences removed, taken for the `cargo-test` adapter from the first failure block and the `test result:` line, and otherwise from the last non-blank lines of standard error and then standard output; any of them that matches REQ-records-006 SHALL be redacted, never refused, and the entry SHALL say how many were. `chough evidence add --failing-lines <text>` SHALL keep the same for a manual failure, within the same bounds and checked as REQ-records-006 checks other text. `chough evidence add --artifact <path>` and `chough cases record --artifact <path>` SHALL accept an absolute path outside the project: Chough SHALL fingerprint it in place with SHA-256 within a 64 MiB bound and SHALL NOT copy it into the repository; a missing path, a directory, a symlink and a file over 64 MiB SHALL be refused, naming the path on standard error only. It SHALL record the path and fingerprint in `.chough/local/external-artifacts.toml` under a neutral ID `ext-<6 lowercase hex>` from fresh random bits, written by create-new, and the committed entry SHALL give only `external: ext-<hex>` and `fingerprint: sha256:<64 hex>`. On the machine that holds the ID, freshness SHALL re-read the file and report the artifact `current` or `changed`; elsewhere it SHALL be reported `external, not checkable here`, which SHALL be neither a pass nor a staleness of the entry's own content identity. A path outside the project, a local tool path and a raw log SHALL be kept only in `.chough/local/`. Key screenshots are committed under REQ-records-013. Trace: KNOW-35, LIFE-19, FLOW-22, LIFE-26, LIFE-8.

Acceptance Criteria
- `chough cases run` writes its captured output to `.chough/local/logs/`, and the committed entry carries the exact command and the output's SHA-256; no file Git tracks under `.chough/` holds a raw log.
- A failing `cargo-test` case's entry keeps its panic lines and its `test result:` line, at most 20 lines and 2,048 bytes, with no escape sequence; a failing line holding a home path is stored as `<redacted:home-path>` and the entry counts it.
- An absolute artifact outside the project is fingerprinted in place, its path appears only in `.chough/local/external-artifacts.toml`, and the committed entry holds only the neutral ID and the fingerprint.
- A missing path, a directory, a symlink and a 64 MiB plus one byte file are refused with nothing recorded.
- Changing the external file makes the artifact read `changed` on that machine; with `.chough/local/` removed the entry reads `external, not checkable here` and is neither reported passed nor stale because of that artifact.

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-006

No leaks in committed records. Before writing any committed record, Chough SHALL check every value it writes, in front matter and body, including recorded commands, summaries, reasons, decisions, relayed words and places, notes, limitations, titles and actor values, for: an absolute path under a home, temporary or system root (`/Users/`, `/home/`, `/root/`, `/private/`, `/tmp/`, `/var/`, `/Volumes/`, `/mnt/`) or a Windows drive path; a `~/` path; the local host name, and the local user name when it appears inside a path or as `<user>@<host>`; an email address; and credentials: GitHub, GitLab, Slack, AWS and model-provider token forms, private key blocks, bearer and basic authorization values, JSON web tokens, and `password`, `secret`, `token` or `api_key` assignments with a value of 8 or more characters. A value that matches SHALL refuse the write as `refused:leak`, naming the field and the kind of match and never the matched text, with every file byte-identical. With `--redact`, each match SHALL instead be replaced by `<redacted:<kind>>` and the write SHALL proceed, saying how many values were redacted. A human's configured display name (REQ-records-008) SHALL NOT count as a user name. `chough doctor` SHALL scan every file Git tracks under `.chough/` and `knowledge/` for the same patterns and SHALL report each as a `leak` finding with the file, the line and the kind, never the text; the verify and CI lanes SHALL run that scan and fail on a finding. Trace: LIFE-26, LIFE-17, FLOW-22.

Acceptance Criteria
- Each pattern kind, placed in an evidence summary, a decision, a relayed `--words`, a bug note and a recorded command, refuses the write with `refused:leak` naming the field and kind, the output never contains the matched text, and every file is unchanged.
- The same writes with `--redact` store `<redacted:<kind>>` and report the count.
- A decision naming the configured human display name is written; the same name inside `/Users/<name>/` is refused.
- With one committed record holding a home path, `chough doctor` reports a `leak` finding with its file, line and kind, without the path, and exits 1; `fledge lanes run verify` fails on it.
- The records of a full fixture cycle run inside a temporary directory hold no absolute path, user name or host name.

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-007

The acting agent. Each timeline entry (REQ-history-002), evidence entry and bug SHALL record the acting agent when it is known, as `actor` with `agent`, `model` and `terminal`, each only when known: `CHOUGH_ACTOR`, `CHOUGH_ACTOR_MODEL` and `CHOUGH_HC_TERMINAL` SHALL set them; otherwise the agent SHALL be `claude-code` when `CLAUDECODE` is set and the terminal SHALL be `HC_TERMINAL_ID` when it is set. The terminal SHALL be the hc terminal address or name, such as `t370`, and never a file path, a host name or a network address. A value that fails the check of REQ-records-006 SHALL be left out with a warning on standard error, and an unknown actor SHALL leave the field out; neither SHALL fail a command. `chough next` and `chough status` SHALL show the last actor, and `chough status` SHALL show each listed evidence entry's actor. The actor is an unauthenticated claim and SHALL never stand in for a human decision (FLOW-16). Trace: LIFE-17, LIFE-26, FLOW-16.

Acceptance Criteria
- With the three override variables set, a stage move, an evidence add and a bug add each record that agent, model and terminal; with only `CLAUDECODE` and `HC_TERMINAL_ID` set they record `claude-code` and that terminal.
- With none set, no record has `actor`, `chough next` shows no last actor, and every command succeeds.
- A terminal value holding a home path is left out with a warning and the command succeeds.
- The shared test fixture clears the five variables, so the suite gives the same records under Claude Code and hc.

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-008

A human's gate approval drives the native approval as that human. Chough SHALL read human identities from configuration, never from its source: the user configuration file `chough/identity.toml` under `XDG_CONFIG_HOME` (by default `~/.config`), overridden by `.chough/local/identity.toml` when present, each `[[human]]` giving `name`, the display name records show, optional `aliases`, and `specsync_actor`, the actor SpecSync records. When a gate that drives a native step (REQ-cli-030) is closed with a human's decision (REQ-cli-050, REQ-cli-059, REQ-cli-061), Chough SHALL resolve `--decided-by` to one configured human by name or alias and SHALL run, before it records anything, `specsync change approve <id> --actor <specsync_actor> --note <note>` for a gate that drives approval, the note giving the human's exact words, where and when they decided, and the agent that relayed or recorded it, or `specsync change review <id> --reviewer <specsync_actor> --verdict <pass or block>` for one that drives review, with the verdict given as `--verdict` on the close, which SHALL be required there and SHALL never be read from the words. A human decision that is neither an approval nor a review, such as a request for changes, SHALL be recorded with `--no-native`, which closes the gate with the relay and runs nothing; Chough SHALL NOT decide which case applies from the words. When SpecSync refuses, Chough SHALL pass its output and exit code through and record nothing; when it succeeds, Chough SHALL close the gate with the relay and add the decision, which SHALL name the native step run. `--decided-by` naming no configured human, or two, SHALL be refused as `refused:human-identity-unknown`, naming both configuration files and the keys to add, before anything runs; Chough SHALL NOT guess an identity or fall back to an agent account. A gate that drives no native step SHALL record no human. An agent that approves or reviews on authority the human gave in advance, without the human answering that gate, SHALL record the native step as the acting agent, `<agent>-<terminal>` (REQ-cli-029), quoting the human's earlier words and where they said them, so only a decision made at that gate is in the human's name (FLOW-24). No source file, skill, template or document SHALL name a specific person as the default human. The relay remains an unauthenticated claim (FLOW-16). Trace: FLOW-23, FLOW-24, FLOW-16, FLOW-10, LIFE-26.

Acceptance Criteria
- With a configured human and a scope gate that drives approval, closing it with that human's relay runs `specsync change approve` once with the configured actor and a note holding the words, place and relaying agent, then closes the gate and records the decision.
- The same at an acceptance gate with `--verdict pass` runs `specsync change review --reviewer` as that human with that verdict, and without `--verdict` it refuses before anything runs; `--no-native` closes a driving gate with the relay and starts no SpecSync process.
- A SpecSync refusal's output and exit code pass through, the gate stays open and nothing is written.
- An unconfigured or ambiguous `--decided-by` refuses with `refused:human-identity-unknown` naming both files, and no SpecSync process starts.
- `.chough/local/identity.toml` overrides the user file; a gate that drives nothing closes with no native call.
- An approval or review on authority given in advance names the agent account `<agent>-<terminal>` as the SpecSync actor and quotes the human's earlier words and place, and never the human as the actor.
- A search of `src/`, `skills/`, `docs/` and `README.md` finds no person's name or account given as a default identity.

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-009

Migration from 0.5.x. `chough migrate` SHALL read a project's 0.5.x records, write nothing, and print the plan: each 0.5.x file with its SHA-256 and what it becomes, each 0.6.0 file it would write, every value it would redact by kind (REQ-records-006), and every file it would remove without carrying it, with why, and SHALL end with the plan's SHA-256. `chough migrate --apply <plan digest>` SHALL refuse, writing nothing, unless the plan it builds has that digest, so nothing is migrated that was not previewed first; it SHALL also refuse, writing nothing, while a 0.5.x file to be removed differs from its bytes at HEAD or is untracked, naming each, so Git holds every byte it removes. It SHALL then write the 0.6.0 tree by create-new only and SHALL refuse, writing nothing more, when a file it would write already exists with other bytes; it SHALL read each written file back and compare its digest; it SHALL write the receipt `.chough/archive/migrations/<YYYY-MM-DD>-from-0-5.md` listing, for every file before and after, its path, size and SHA-256, the redactions by file, field and kind, every removed file with why and the commit that holds it, and the old-to-new ID map; and only then SHALL it remove the 0.5.x files, each only when its bytes still match the plan. It SHALL never weaken a SpecSync archive: before removing anything it SHALL read the native validity of every archived change whose acceptance manifest lists a file it would remove, at most 8 reads at once, read them again after the removal, and, if any archive that read valid no longer does, restore every removed file byte for byte and refuse, naming those archives. A file written by an interrupted apply with the planned bytes SHALL count as done on the next apply, and a second apply on a migrated tree SHALL report nothing to do. The mapping SHALL be: each open session, legacy `.chough/session.json` included, becomes a session folder whose timeline holds, in order, every transition of its selected history lineage, the legacy log's entries going to the session that adopted them; each archived session, and each session rolled into `knowledge/attachments/`, becomes an archived story; each observation an open session references becomes an evidence entry of that session, each one an archived session or a bug references becomes a receipt of that story or bug, and others are removed; a PNG or JPEG artifact of a carried observation that fits the bounds of REQ-records-013 is kept beside its session or story, and other artifacts are removed; each bug becomes a Markdown bug; inbox entries move to their session's `agent/inbox/`; every 0.5.x ID is kept as an alias (REQ-records-003), and an ID whose record was removed without being carried SHALL resolve as `removed`, naming the receipt, never as current or passed; and `.chough/transitions.jsonl`, `.chough/history/`, `.chough/pruned.jsonl`, `.chough/session.v1.json`, `.chough/artifacts/` other than files an open bug names, shelved 0.5.x sessions and the generated or copied vault content of REQ-cli-044 are removed after they are listed. Every file migration writes SHALL pass REQ-records-006 with redaction, so no home-folder path, user name or credential survives in a rewritten record; a file it keeps byte for byte (an open bug's fixture, a `library/` snapshot or brief) that matches SHALL be listed in the plan and the receipt as a `leak` finding for a person to fix, never changed silently and never kept unreported. The receipt SHALL say that Git history still holds what earlier commits leaked, which this migration does not rewrite. Case definitions and preferences SHALL stay byte-identical. Removal SHALL follow REQ-records-012. Trace: DRIVE-27, LIFE-20, LIFE-21, LIFE-8, KNOW-30, KNOW-31, KNOW-34, LIFE-26.

Acceptance Criteria
- Fixtures captured from a project at every released 0.x version, 0.1.0 to 0.5.0, each built from its release tag, migrate to the same kind of 0.6.0 tree with every record readable (#213).
- `--apply` without a digest, or with the digest of an earlier plan, refuses and writes nothing; `--apply` with the preview's digest proceeds.
- With a SpecSync stub whose archive reads invalid once a listed file is gone, `--apply` restores every removed file byte for byte and refuses naming that archive.
- A rewritten record that held a home-folder path holds `<redacted:home-path>` and the receipt counts it; a kept fixture holding one is listed as a `leak` finding and left unchanged; the receipt says Git history is not rewritten.
- On a 0.5.x fixture with a legacy checkpoint, adopted history, two open and seven archived sessions, rolled sessions in the vault, a bug of each ID form, inbox entries and old proof, `chough migrate` writes nothing and lists every file, target, redaction and removal.
- `--apply` with an uncommitted or untracked 0.5.x file refuses naming it, with nothing written.
- `--apply` writes every planned file and the receipt, removes the 0.5.x files, and afterwards every record, alias and timeline entry is readable by `next`, `status`, `bug show`, `session list` and `timing`; the receipt's before and after digests match the files at the earlier and the new commit.
- With a target that already exists with other bytes, `--apply` refuses and writes nothing more; after an apply interrupted between writes, the next apply completes and the result equals an uninterrupted one; a second apply on the result reports nothing to do.
- Case definition files and `.chough/preferences.json` are byte-identical before and after.
- Every transition of the selected lineage appears once, in order, in its session's timeline, and every archived session, the rolled ones included, is an archived story.
- A bug linking an observation that was removed without being carried shows it as `removed` with the receipt named, and `chough next` never counts it as current or passed.
- Regression for the local bug where history writes refused once the adopting session rolled into the vault: a 0.5.x fixture whose adopting session sits in `knowledge/attachments/`, with `.chough/transitions.jsonl` unchanged, migrates; the legacy entries land in that session's archived story, and afterwards `checkpoint --awaiting-human`, `--close-gate` and a stage move all succeed. The same writes are first recorded refusing on 0.5.0.

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-010

Fail closed on an unmigrated tree. When a project holds any 0.5.x record (`.chough/session.json`, `.chough/sessions/*.json`, `.chough/sessions/archive/`, `.chough/history/`, `.chough/transitions.jsonl`, `.chough/evidence/`, a `.chough/bugs/*.json` bug or `.chough/pruned.jsonl`), every command other than `chough migrate`, `chough doctor`, `chough skill`, `--help` and `--version` SHALL refuse as `refused:unmigrated`, writing nothing, with a message that names up to 5 such paths and the count of the rest, says the project uses the Chough 0.5.x layout, and names `chough migrate` to preview and `chough migrate --apply` to convert. A mixed tree, with 0.6.0 records and a 0.5.x file written after migration, such as by an older Chough, SHALL be refused the same way, and `chough doctor` SHALL report that file. Trace: LIFE-20, DRIVE-25, LIFE-3.

Acceptance Criteria
- On an unmigrated fixture, `next`, `status`, `stage advance`, `checkpoint`, `evidence add`, `bug add`, `cases run` and `init` each refuse with `refused:unmigrated` naming `chough migrate`, and every file is byte-identical.
- With 12 such paths the message names 5 and says 7 more.
- `chough migrate`, `chough doctor`, `chough skill status`, `--help` and `--version` run on the same fixture.
- A `.chough/session.json` written into a migrated tree makes the next command refuse, and `chough doctor` reports it as `unmigrated`.

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-011

Journal and feed. The knowledge vault's journal SHALL be dated Markdown posts in `knowledge/journal/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>.md`, each with front matter giving `id`, `title`, `type: journal`, `tags`, `date`, `kind` (`shipped`, `closed`, `intent`, `spec`, `broke` or `learned`), `links` and, for a finished session, `record`, the path of its archived story. When a session archives itself at its move into done (REQ-history-006), Chough SHALL write a `shipped` post, or a `closed` post for a session closed without done, by create-new: the title is the session's objective, the body is the story's first Outcome or Where it stands paragraph cut to at most 1,200 bytes at a sentence end, followed by a line linking the PR when the session names one, the archived story and, when the session's native change is archived, its `.specsync/archive/changes/` folder, so the post is short and the story holds the full record. The journal and its feed SHALL be the timeline of archived changes: `knowledge/index.md` SHALL present them as that timeline and SHALL link `.specsync/archive/changes/` for changes archived before the journal began (REQ-cli-043), and no other timeline page SHALL be generated. `chough journal add --kind <kind> --title <title> --body <text> [--link <link>]...` SHALL write any other post by create-new, refusing a body over 1,200 bytes. Chough SHALL never rewrite or remove a post, so a person's edit is kept. `knowledge/journal/feed.xml` SHALL be an Atom feed of the newest 20 posts by date and then name, generated from the posts with identical bytes for the same posts, and `chough library index` SHALL regenerate it, with `--check` covering it (REQ-cli-047). The journal SHALL copy no record: it links them. Trace: KNOW-33, KNOW-30, KNOW-31, KNOW-15, KNOW-4, KNOW-14.

Acceptance Criteria

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-012

Removal only of committed bytes. No Chough command SHALL remove a committed record or working file, or move it out of its folder, which covers a session's `session.md`, its `evidence/` entries, screenshots and `agent/` files, a 0.5.x file during migration, a shelved session and an archived story, unless that file's exact bytes are committed at the current commit (HEAD). Rewriting a record in place under REQ-records-001 is not a removal. A file Chough would remove whose bytes differ from HEAD, or that HEAD does not hold, SHALL be kept in place and reported with its path and the reason, and the command SHALL say how to finish once the file is committed. No Chough command SHALL remove or rewrite an archived story's existing bytes, and nothing SHALL trim `.chough/archive/sessions/` by count or age: every finished session stays as one story file, with its screenshot folder when it has one, so links to it never break; a correction only appends (REQ-history-015). ### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-013

Key screenshots beside the story. `chough evidence add --screenshot <path>` SHALL commit one small key screenshot with its evidence entry: a PNG or JPEG file of at most 524,288 bytes, at most 5 per session and at most 2,097,152 bytes per session in all. ### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-014

Chough's records stay out of SpecSync's verification. The folders Chough writes while work is in progress, `.chough/sessions/`, `.chough/archive/` and `.chough/bugs/`, SHALL hold only Chough's records: no case definition, preference, reading guide or other input that a change's verification needs SHALL be written under them, and `.chough/cases/`, `.chough/preferences.json`, `.chough/README.md` and `knowledge/` SHALL stay outside them. These three prefixes are what a project lists in SpecSync's documented exclusion, `verification_ignored_paths` in `.specsync/sdd.json` (SpecSync 6.1.2 and later), so that a Chough record written after `specsync change check --commit`, whether left untracked or committed, leaves the recorded verification current, in the author's tree and in a reviewer's clean checkout alike; `.chough/local/` is already Git-ignored and never part of it. Chough SHALL read that policy and SHALL never write it: `chough doctor` SHALL report `records_in_verification` (REQ-history-012) while `.specsync/sdd.json` exists and its list lacks any of the three prefixes, naming the missing entries, the SpecSync version that supports them and that adding them is an ordinary reviewed change to `.specsync/sdd.json`; `chough init` SHALL print the same advice; and the migration plan and receipt SHALL list it as a follow-up (REQ-records-009). `chough next` SHALL say when Chough writes are safe: with the three entries in force, that Chough's records may be written and committed at any point of the native order; without them, that Chough's records are committed before `change check --commit` and nothing under `.chough/` is written until finalize. When SpecSync refuses the list because an open change's affected paths overlap it, Chough SHALL pass that refusal through and name the change. Trace: DRIVE-4, FLOW-16.

Acceptance Criteria
- With the three entries listed and real SpecSync at the CI pin, an untracked evidence entry present while `change check --commit` runs leaves verification current in that tree and in a clean clone of the pushed commit, whose workspace digest matches the recorded one (spec-sync #1045's case); the same steps without the entries leave the clone's verification stale, as the control.
- With the three entries listed, a `chough checkpoint`, a `chough evidence add` and a `chough bug add` written after `change check --commit`, and then committed, leave `specsync change status` reporting verification current and the audit passing, and the review then records (the #212 case); without the entries the same writes make verification stale, as the control.
- A case definition changed under `.chough/cases/` after `change check --commit` still makes verification stale, so nothing a change's verification needs is hidden.
- With a prefix missing, `chough doctor` reports `records_in_verification` naming the missing entries and writes nothing; with all three listed it reports nothing; `.specsync/sdd.json` is byte-identical after every Chough command.
- `chough next` says Chough's writes are safe at any point with the entries in force, and gives the commit-first order without them.
