## MODIFIED

### REQUIREMENT REQ-cli-012

Local bug backlog. The helper SHALL keep each bug as one Markdown record, `.chough/bugs/<id>.md` (REQ-records-001), whose front matter gives `kind: bug`, `id`, `aliases`, `title`, `status`, `classification`, `scope`, `impact`, `criteria`, `requirements`, `cases`, `evidence` (the linked evidence IDs), `receipts` (the receipt fields of REQ-history-006 for linked evidence no longer in an open session), `regression_baseline`, `fixture`, `environment`, `revision`, `found_by` (the actor, REQ-records-007), `fixed_in`, `duplicate_of`, `related`, `created` and `updated`, where `regression_baseline`, `fixture`, `fixed_in` and `duplicate_of` are written only when set and a missing one reads as empty, and whose body gives `# <title>` and the paragraphs What happened (the symptom and actual behavior), Steps (the trigger, labelled `Trigger:`, then each step), Expected, Why it matters and Fix (`Not fixed yet.` until `chough bug update --fix <text>` sets it, each set also logged; marking a bug verified while its Fix still says `Not fixed yet.` SHALL replace that with a line naming the passing observation and its revision), then `## Log`, an append-only list with one dated entry per report, note, evidence link and status change. A Log entry SHALL name each piece of linked evidence as a Markdown link relative to the bug's file, to the open session's entry (`../sessions/<session>/evidence/<id>.md`) or to the archived story that holds it as a receipt (`../archive/sessions/<session>.md`), and by its name alone when only the bug holds it, while the front matter keeps the stored form. It SHALL list open bugs for a fresh session and SHALL search existing records by symptom, trigger and affected IDs before a new record is added. A bug SHALL remain unverified until a failed observation is linked, and a regression classification SHALL require a passed observation at an ancestor revision. A bug SHALL read as reproduced and later passed, and SHALL accept the verified status, only when a present linked passed observation was observed strictly after its most recent present linked failed observation; a receipt counts as present, and an observation shown as removed (REQ-records-009) does not. Additional evidence SHALL be appended to the Log, never overwriting anything. `chough bug add` while a session is active SHALL also add the new bug to that session's checkpoint (`checkpoint --bug`, REQ-records-004), with or without `--observation`, so the session's story names the bugs found in it and a blocking bug filed in it stops it (REQ-cli-054); with no session active, it SHALL leave every checkpoint unchanged. `chough bug update` given `--observation` with an evidence entry of the active session SHALL also add the bug to that session's checkpoint. A bug in another tool's behaviour MAY be closed as `fixed-upstream` with `bug update --status fixed-upstream --note <text>`, the note naming the upstream fix and who checked it where; it is a closed status, as `verified`, `duplicate` and `wont-fix` are, needs no Chough observation, and SHALL never read as verified by Chough. The helper SHALL render a report candidate and SHALL NOT publish it. Every new bug SHALL be named by REQ-records-003 from its title, the same for manual `bug add` and automatic failure capture, and written by create-new; an update SHALL NOT rename the record, even when its title has changed. Every `BUG-NNNN` and `BUG-<slug>-<hex>` ID from 0.5.x SHALL resolve as an alias, and duplicate and related links SHALL resolve in either direction across aliases and new names. Documentation SHALL state that Chough 0.5.x cannot read 0.6.0 bug records. Trace: FLOW-8, LIFE-5, LIFE-8, DRIVE-25, DRIVE-27.

Acceptance Criteria
- A sample failure becomes a reproduced and classified bug; a second report of the same symptom is found by search and its evidence is appended to the Log.
- A regression classification without a passed observation at an ancestor revision is refused.
- An out-of-scope bug stays in the open backlog without changing the checkpoint scope.
- Rendering a report candidate prints Markdown and performs no network action.
- A pass observed before, or in the same second as, the latest linked failure leaves the bug reproduced and the verified status is refused; a bug with no linked failure cannot be marked verified.
- `--classification blocking` is refused with a hint to use `--scope`, and `--scope product-defect` with a hint to use `--classification`.
- Manual `bug add` and automatic failure capture both name the bug `<date>-<title slug>`, and changing a stored title and then updating its status or evidence leaves the name unchanged.
- Each update appends one Log entry and leaves every earlier Log line, and every body paragraph a person wrote, byte-identical.
- A migrated bug answers to its `BUG-NNNN` alias in list, search, show, update and report, and duplicate and related links resolve from alias to new name and back.
- After the session holding a bug's linked evidence is archived, the bug resolves that evidence to the story's receipt and its verified state is unchanged.
- The CLI documentation says Chough 0.5.x cannot read 0.6.0 bug records.
- `bug update --status fixed-upstream` without a note is refused; with one it closes the bug, `bug list` shows it closed as fixed upstream, and nothing reports it verified.
- A new bug's Steps start with `Trigger:` and the trigger; `bug update --fix <text>` sets the Fix paragraph and logs it; a bug marked verified with no Fix set says which observation passed at which revision instead of `Not fixed yet.`
- A bug added while a session is active, with or without `--observation`, is in that session's checkpoint and in its archived story; a bug added with no session active is in no checkpoint.
- A bug updated with `--observation` naming an evidence entry of the active session is in that session's checkpoint; one naming another session's entry leaves the active checkpoint unchanged.
