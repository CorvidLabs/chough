A file written by an interrupted apply with the planned bytes SHALL count as done on the next apply, and a second apply on a migrated tree SHALL report nothing to do; on a tree with no 0.5.x record the preview SHALL likewise say there is nothing to migrate and give no plan digest. Every file migration writes SHALL pass REQ-records-006 with redaction, so no home-folder path, user name or credential survives in a rewritten record; a file it keeps byte for byte (an open bug's fixture, a `library/` snapshot or brief) that matches SHALL be listed in the plan and the receipt as a `leak` finding for a person to fix, never changed silently and never kept unreported. The receipt SHALL say that Git history still holds what earlier commits leaked, which this migration does not rewrite. Case definitions and preferences SHALL stay byte-identical. Removal SHALL follow REQ-records-012. Trace: DRIVE-27, LIFE-20, LIFE-21, LIFE-8, KNOW-10, KNOW-11, KNOW-30, KNOW-31, KNOW-34, LIFE-26.

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-011

Journal and feed. The knowledge vault's journal SHALL be dated Markdown posts in `knowledge/journal/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>.md`, each with front matter giving `id`, `title`, `type: journal`, `tags`, `date`, `time` (the UTC time it was written, ISO 8601 to the second; the file name keeps only the date), `kind` (`shipped`, `closed`, `intent`, `spec`, `broke` or `learned`), `links` and, for a finished session, `record`, the path of its archived story. `chough checkpoint --title <title>` SHALL record the session's post title, one line of at most 72 bytes, as `title`, and `chough checkpoint --summary <text>` its post summary of at most 900 bytes as `summary`, one list item per non-blank line of the text; each SHALL replace the earlier value, an empty value SHALL remove it, both SHALL pass REQ-records-006, and the story SHALL keep both, so the agent who did the work writes the post's short human title and its summary before the move into done. When a session archives itself at its move into done (REQ-history-006), Chough SHALL write a `shipped` post, or a `closed` post for a session closed without done, by create-new. Its title SHALL be the session's `title`, or, when it has none, a title derived from its objective: the text before the first `: `, `; `, `, `, ` (` or ` — ` that starts at least 16 bytes in, cut at a word to at most 72 bytes and ending in `…` when cut. Its body SHALL be the session's `summary`, one paragraph per line, or, when it has none, a body derived from the story: the Goal's first paragraph, then `Why:` with the first Decisions and why entry, then `Verified:` with the newest What was verified entry, then `Next:` with the first Open threads entry, an empty part left out. The body SHALL be cut at a sentence end so that it stays within 1,200 bytes with the line that follows it, a line linking the PR when the session names one, the archived story and, when the session's native change is archived, its `.specsync/archive/changes/` folder, so the post is short and the story holds the full record. `chough journal add --record <story> --title <title> --body <text> [--link <link>]...` SHALL write the post of an archived story that has none, for a backfill of stories archived before the journal began or before this rule: its `kind` SHALL be `shipped` when the story's `outcome` is `done` and `closed` otherwise, its `date` the story's `closed` date, its `time` the latest time among the story's Timeline entries when that time falls on that date (otherwise it records none), its `record` the story, and its links those of the post at the move into done followed by each `--link`; it SHALL refuse, writing nothing, when the story does not exist or when a post's `record` already names it, naming that post. The journal and its feed SHALL be the timeline of archived changes: `knowledge/index.md` SHALL present them as that timeline and SHALL link `.specsync/archive/changes/` for changes archived before the journal began (REQ-cli-043), and no other timeline page SHALL be generated. `chough journal add --kind <kind> --title <title> --body <text> [--link <link>]...` SHALL write any other post by create-new, refusing a body over 1,200 bytes. Chough SHALL never rewrite or remove a post, so a person's edit is kept. `knowledge/journal/feed.xml` SHALL be an Atom feed of the newest 20 posts, newest first by `time`. A post without a `time`, such as a migrated or hand-written one, SHALL sort by its date and then its name, after the timed posts of the same date, and the index SHALL mark it "(no time)". The index's newest 10 posts SHALL use the same order. An entry's `<updated>` SHALL be its `time` when it has one, and the feed's `<updated>` the first entry's. The feed SHALL be generated from the posts with identical bytes for the same posts, and `chough library index` SHALL regenerate it, with `--check` covering it (REQ-cli-047). The journal SHALL copy no record: it links them. Trace: KNOW-33, KNOW-30, KNOW-31, KNOW-15, KNOW-4, KNOW-14.

Acceptance Criteria
- The move into done of a session with a PR and an archived native change writes one `shipped` post whose body is at most 1,200 bytes and links the PR, `.chough/archive/sessions/<id>.md` and the change's archive folder, and `feed.xml` lists it.
- `knowledge/index.md` names the journal and feed as the timeline of archived changes and links `.specsync/archive/changes/`, and no `timeline.md` or `timeline/` is generated.
- A person's edit to a post survives a later move into done and `library index`, and a post name that already exists is never overwritten.
- `chough journal add` writes a post of each kind, and a body over 1,200 bytes is refused with nothing written.
- Regenerating `feed.xml` twice gives identical bytes, it parses as XML, and `library index --check` fails naming it after a hand edit.
- Each new post records `time`. Posts written on one day are listed newest first in the index and the feed, a post without a `time` follows that day's timed posts and is marked "(no time)" in the index, and an entry's `<updated>` is its `time`.
- With `chough checkpoint --title` and `--summary` set, the move into done writes a post whose title is that title and whose body holds each summary line; without them, the title is the objective's first clause within 72 bytes and the body holds the Goal, the first decision, the newest verified entry and the first open thread, within 1,200 bytes with its links.
- `chough checkpoint --title` over 72 bytes or holding a line break, and `--summary` over 900 bytes, are refused with nothing written; an empty value removes the field.
- `chough journal add --record <story>` writes one post dated by the story's `closed`, with the story's latest Timeline time on that date, its `record` and links, of kind `shipped` for a done story and `closed` otherwise; a second `--record` for the same story, or one naming no archived story, is refused with nothing written.

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-006

No leaks in committed records. Before writing any committed record, Chough SHALL check every value it writes, in front matter and body, including recorded commands, summaries, reasons, decisions, relayed words and places, notes, limitations, titles and actor values, for: an absolute path under a home, temporary or system root (`/Users/`, `/home/`, `/root/`, `/private/`, `/tmp/`, `/var/`, `/Volumes/`, `/mnt/`) or a Windows drive path; a `~/` path; the local host name, and the local user name when it appears inside a path or as `<user>@<host>`; an email address; and credentials: GitHub, GitLab, Slack, AWS and model-provider token forms, private key blocks, bearer and basic authorization values, JSON web tokens, and `password`, `secret`, `token` or `api_key` assignments with a value of 8 or more characters. A value that matches SHALL refuse the write as `refused:leak`, naming the field and the kind of match and never the matched text, with every file byte-identical. With `--redact`, each match SHALL instead be replaced by `<redacted:<kind>>` and the write SHALL proceed, saying how many values were redacted. A human's configured display name (REQ-records-008) SHALL NOT count as a user name. `chough doctor` SHALL scan every file Git tracks under `.chough/` and `knowledge/` for the same patterns and SHALL report each as a `leak` finding with the file, the line and the kind, never the text, except that a path under `/tmp/` or `/private/tmp/` in a case definition (`.chough/cases/*.cases.toml`) is a deliberate test setting, such as a case's own `XDG_DATA_HOME`, and SHALL NOT be reported or offered `--redact`, while a home path, the local user or host name, an email or a credential in a case definition still SHALL; the verify and CI lanes SHALL run that scan and fail on a finding. Trace: LIFE-26, LIFE-17, FLOW-22.

Acceptance Criteria
- Each pattern kind, placed in an evidence summary, a decision, a relayed `--words`, a bug note and a recorded command, refuses the write with `refused:leak` naming the field and kind, the output never contains the matched text, and every file is unchanged.
- The same writes with `--redact` store `<redacted:<kind>>` and report the count.
- A decision naming the configured human display name is written; the same name inside `/Users/<name>/` is refused.
- With one committed record holding a home path, `chough doctor` reports a `leak` finding with its file, line and kind, without the path, and exits 1; `fledge lanes run verify` fails on it.
- The records of a full fixture cycle run inside a temporary directory hold no absolute path, user name or host name.
- A committed case definition whose binding sets `XDG_DATA_HOME` to a path under `/tmp/` gives no `leak` finding from `chough doctor`, while one holding a home-folder path still gives one naming the file and line.

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-008

A human's gate approval drives the native approval as that human. Chough SHALL read human identities from configuration, never from its source: the user configuration file `chough/identity.toml` under `XDG_CONFIG_HOME` (by default `~/.config`), overridden by `.chough/local/identity.toml` when present, each `[[human]]` giving `name`, the display name records show, optional `aliases`, and `specsync_actor`, the actor SpecSync records. Every close of a gate with a human's decision (REQ-cli-050, REQ-cli-059, REQ-cli-061), whatever the gate drives and with or without `--no-native`, SHALL resolve `--decided-by` to one configured human by name or alias before anything runs or is written. When the gate drives a native step (REQ-cli-030), Chough SHALL then run, before it records anything, `specsync change approve <id> --actor <specsync_actor> --note <note>` for a gate that drives approval, the note giving the human's exact words, where and when they decided, and the agent that relayed or recorded it, or `specsync change review <id> --reviewer <specsync_actor> --verdict <pass or block>` for one that drives review, with the verdict given as `--verdict` on the close, which SHALL be required there and SHALL never be read from the words. A human decision that is neither an approval nor a review, such as a request for changes, SHALL be recorded with `--no-native`, which closes the gate with the relay and runs nothing; Chough SHALL NOT decide which case applies from the words. When SpecSync refuses, Chough SHALL pass its output and exit code through and record nothing; when it succeeds, Chough SHALL close the gate with the relay and add the decision, which SHALL name the native step run. `--decided-by` naming no configured human, or two, SHALL be refused as `refused:human-identity-unknown`, naming both configuration files and the keys to add, before anything runs; Chough SHALL NOT guess an identity or fall back to an agent account. A gate that drives no native step SHALL run no native step, and its decision is still in the configured human's name. An agent that approves or reviews on authority the human gave in advance, without the human answering that gate, SHALL record the native step as the acting agent, `<agent>-<terminal>` (REQ-cli-029), quoting the human's earlier words and where they said them, so only a decision made at that gate is in the human's name (FLOW-24). No source file, skill, template or document SHALL name a specific person as the default human. The relay remains an unauthenticated claim (FLOW-16). Trace: FLOW-23, FLOW-24, FLOW-16, FLOW-10, LIFE-26.

Acceptance Criteria
- With a configured human and a scope gate that drives approval, closing it with that human's relay runs `specsync change approve` once with the configured actor and a note holding the words, place and relaying agent, then closes the gate and records the decision.
- The same at an acceptance gate with `--verdict pass` runs `specsync change review --reviewer` as that human with that verdict, and without `--verdict` it refuses before anything runs; `--no-native` closes a driving gate with the relay and starts no SpecSync process.
- A SpecSync refusal's output and exit code pass through, the gate stays open and nothing is written.
- An unconfigured or ambiguous `--decided-by` refuses with `refused:human-identity-unknown` naming both files, and no SpecSync process starts.
- `.chough/local/identity.toml` overrides the user file; a gate that drives nothing closes with no native call.
- An approval or review on authority given in advance names the agent account `<agent>-<terminal>` as the SpecSync actor and quotes the human's earlier words and place, and never the human as the actor.
- A search of `src/`, `skills/`, `docs/` and `README.md` finds no person's name or account given as a default identity.
- With no identity file, or one that does not name `--decided-by`, closing an intent gate that drives nothing, closing a driving gate with `--no-native`, and the same through `chough gate record` and `chough change ship --close-gate`, each refuse with `refused:human-identity-unknown` before anything runs, with every file unchanged; for a configured human each succeeds.
