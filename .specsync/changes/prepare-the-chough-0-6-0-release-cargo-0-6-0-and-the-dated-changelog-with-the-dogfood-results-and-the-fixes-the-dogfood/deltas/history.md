## MODIFIED

### REQUIREMENT REQ-history-006

Sessions in their own folders. `chough session new <key>` SHALL create `.chough/sessions/<id>/session.md` by create-new, where `<id>` is the date and the slug of `<key>` (REQ-records-003) and `<key>` is kept as an alias, with its own timeline, and SHALL point at it; `session use <id>` SHALL point at an open session by ID, alias or the bare slug when exactly one open session has it; an active pointer that holds such an alias or bare slug, as a 0.5.x pointer that an in-place migration keeps, SHALL name that session, and `chough doctor` SHALL NOT report it as `stale_pointer`; `session list` SHALL report each session's ID, aliases, stage, status (`archived` for an archived story, otherwise its stage status) and whether it is active, open or archived. A session is finished when it is at stage done or its native change is archived on HEAD, at any stage, as the archive's `finalization.json` records, read without a native read (DRIVE-16). A session SHALL close only itself, at its own move into done or with `chough session close --reason`: `session new` and `session use` SHALL NOT archive, close or change any other session, even a finished one (#207, #214). `session list`, `chough next` and `chough doctor` SHALL list each finished open session with its fix, `chough doctor --fix` (REQ-history-012), which keeps one with unfolded inbox entries or an open awaiting-human gate open and names the recovery: `chough inbox fold`, or `chough checkpoint --close-gate`, since closing a gate is a decision (#181). When `chough stage advance` moves the active session into done and its save succeeds, after the knowledge refresh of REQ-cli-043, the helper SHALL archive that session in the same command and clear the active pointer, reporting `applied.session_archive` with status `archived` and the ID; a session with unfolded inbox entries SHALL stay open with its pointer, reported as `kept_open` with the count; a session for which release is authorized at that move (a run consent or a standing preference, as `stage enter release` requires) SHALL stay open for the release, reported as `kept_open` with that reason, and SHALL archive itself the same way at the move from release into done. The step SHALL NOT undo the move into done. To archive a session, Chough SHALL write `.chough/archive/sessions/<id>.md` by create-new: the session's front matter with `stage: done` (or the stage it closed at), `closed` (the UTC date) and `outcome` (`done` or `closed-without-done`), and `receipts`, one map per evidence entry the session holds, ordered by its observation time, oldest first, then by ID, with its `id`, `aliases` (one scalar, comma-and-space separated), `claim`, `result`, `observed` (the entry's observation time, ISO UTC, REQ-cli-017), `duration_seconds` (the run's wall-clock seconds, when known), `command`, `revision`, `environment`, `content`, `fingerprint`, `raw_log` when present, `case`, `case_digest`, `screenshot` when present and `actor` when known (REQ-records-007), so a bug whose evidence is archived keeps its verified state, which needs a pass observed after its latest failure (REQ-cli-012), and the timing report can order and time case runs read from receipts (REQ-reporting-001); then the story as written, with one What was verified line per receipt (its claim, its command and the first 12 digits of its fingerprint, ending `^<evidence id>`) for each entry not already listed, the Timeline and a closing line `Closed <date>: <outcome>.` Chough SHALL read the story back and compare it before it removes anything, and SHALL then remove the session folder, `session.md`, `evidence/` and `agent/` included, and move its screenshots beside the story (REQ-records-013), each file only under REQ-records-012; files it keeps SHALL be reported, the folder SHALL count as archived while its story exists, and `chough doctor --fix` SHALL finish the removal once they are committed. Archiving a session SHALL NOT read, move, rewrite or remove any other session's folder or story. After it, `next` and `status` SHALL refuse with "no open session" and name `chough session new <key>` until a session is opened (DRIVE-13). Evidence and bug links into an archived session SHALL resolve to its story's receipts. Two branches that record work in different sessions SHALL merge without conflict. A session that cannot be read, because it is over the bound of REQ-records-001 or invalid, SHALL NOT stop `session list`, `session new`, `session use` or another session's `next`; `session list` SHALL report it with the reason and the recovery `chough session shelve <id>`, which SHALL move the folder unparsed and byte for byte to `.chough/archive/shelved/<id>/` with a `shelved.md` giving its size, SHA-256 per file, time and reason, and clear the active pointer when it named that session. Shelving SHALL never replace a file, and no reader SHALL scan `.chough/archive/shelved/` (DRIVE-14). `chough session close <id> --reason <text>` SHALL close an open session for abandoned work without a native read: it SHALL refuse with nothing changed a blank reason, a session that is archived or does not exist, and a session with unfolded inbox entries, naming `chough session use <id>` and `chough inbox fold`; otherwise it SHALL add the decision `Closed without done: <reason>` and archive the session as above with outcome `closed-without-done`, clearing the active pointer when it named that session (DRIVE-16). Trace: DRIVE-3, DRIVE-24, DRIVE-25, KNOW-30, KNOW-34, LIFE-3, LIFE-7, DRIVE-14, DRIVE-13, DRIVE-16.

Acceptance Criteria
- Two branches that each advance a different session merge in Git with no conflict and keep both sessions' timelines.
- The move into done of the active session writes `.chough/archive/sessions/<id>.md` with every receipt and the full timeline, removes the committed session folder, clears the pointer, reports `archived`, and `next` then refuses naming `chough session new <key>`.
- Each receipt in the story gives its entry's `observed` time, its `duration_seconds`, `raw_log` and `actor` when the entry has them, and its `aliases` as one comma-and-space separated scalar; a bug whose linked pass was observed after its latest linked failure stays verified once that session is archived.
- Regression for issue 165: with seven earlier archived stories present, among them 0.5.x-era stories, and `knowledge/` neither tracked nor ignored, advancing accept to done leaves every earlier story byte-identical in place, `git status` shows no deleted file, and the only new committed record is the new story; the same path in the 0.5.x layout is recorded failing first on the 0.5.0 code and linked to a local bug.
- With an uncommitted file in `agent/`, the move into done writes the story, keeps that file and its folder, reports it, and `next` does not treat the folder as open; after a commit, `doctor --fix` removes the folder.
- At the move into done, a session with unfolded inbox entries stays open (`kept_open` with the count).
- Regression for #207: with a finished session whose native change is archived and that still waits on a publish gate, `session new` and `session use` leave it byte-identical and open, and `next` and `doctor` list it as finished with its fix.
- With release authorized before the move into done, the session stays open (`kept_open`), `stage enter release` works, and the move from release into done archives it.
- A session whose native change is archived on HEAD, at a stage short of done, is listed as finished with no native read, and only `chough doctor --fix` archives it.
- `session list` reports open, active and archived sessions; `session use` accepts the ID, the alias and a unique bare slug.
- With one session over its bound and another unreadable, `session list` reports each with `chough session shelve <id>`, and `next` on the active session and `session new` still work; shelving moves the folder byte for byte under `.chough/archive/shelved/`.
- `chough session close <id> --reason <text>` archives the session with outcome `closed-without-done` and the reason in its decisions; a blank reason, an archived or missing session and a session with unfolded inbox entries refuse with nothing changed.
- After an in-place migration whose machine-local pointer holds a 0.5.x session key, `next` and `status` read that session, and `chough doctor` reports no `stale_pointer`.
- A story's receipts, and its What was verified lines for them, are in observation-time order, oldest first, whatever order the session referenced them in.

### REQUIREMENT REQ-history-002

Append-only timeline. Each session's transitions SHALL be recorded as entries of the `## Timeline` section of its `session.md` (REQ-records-004), and those entries, carried into the archived story at finish, SHALL be the session's only transition history: no event file, cycle directory, cursor or transitions log SHALL be written. Each entry SHALL be one list item, `- <time> · <kind> · <from> → <to> · <text> ^<entry id>`, where `<time>` is the UTC time to the second in ISO 8601, `<kind>` is a transition kind (`advance`, `iterate`, `back`, `reset`, `pause`, `resume`, `skip`, `enter`, `return`, `gate-opened`, `gate-closed`, `direct-native-write`, `correction`), `<text>` is the reason, gate or note, and `<entry id>` is `t-` and 6 lowercase hexadecimal digits from fresh random bits, unique in the file. Its structured fields SHALL follow as indented `- <key>: <value>` lines, plain text and never JSON: a field with parts, such as `relayed_by` and `direct_write`, SHALL be a `- <key>:` line with one `- <part>: <value>` line per part indented under it, and a list of parts one level further; a value that holds a line break, or starts or ends with a space or a `"`, SHALL be written as a JSON string, and Chough SHALL still read the JSON value of an entry written before this rule. The fields are `by` for the actor (REQ-records-007), `native_change` when one is recorded, and the fields of REQ-history-005, REQ-history-010, REQ-history-013, REQ-history-014 and REQ-history-015. The entry and the checkpoint change it goes with SHALL be written in the same atomic replace of `session.md`, so a refused or failed save leaves neither. Chough SHALL only append entries: it SHALL never edit, reorder or remove an existing entry line, and an entry ID SHALL never be reused in its file. A timeline entry is what other requirements call a transition, and the session file's Timeline is what they call its transition log. `chough doctor` SHALL report a session or story whose Timeline at HEAD is not an exact prefix of its Timeline in the working tree (REQ-history-012). Trace: LIFE-2, LIFE-3, LIFE-6, LIFE-7, FLOW-2, FLOW-13, DRIVE-3, DRIVE-24.

Acceptance Criteria
- Every transition kind written in a fixture session appears once, in order, as an entry with its time, kind, stages, text, unique entry ID and structured fields, and no file is written under `.chough/history/` or `.chough/transitions.jsonl`.
- A save refused after its native read leaves `session.md` byte-identical, with no entry and no checkpoint change.
- Two branches that each advance a different session merge with no conflict and keep both timelines.
- Editing an earlier entry by hand and committing nothing else makes `chough doctor` report the session's timeline as rewritten.
- Each session's timeline holds only its own entries, and a stage move in one session leaves every other session's file unchanged.
- A gate closed with a relayed decision and a direct lifecycle write each add an entry whose `relayed_by` and `direct_write` parts are labelled lines with no JSON object, and `history show`, `timing` and `doctor` read both those entries and entries written with JSON values.
