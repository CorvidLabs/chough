## MODIFIED

### REQUIREMENT REQ-history-012

Doctor findings and fixes. `chough doctor` (REQ-cli-055) SHALL find this debt without a native read, and its fixes SHALL use Chough's own steps:
- `unmigrated`: a 0.5.x record (REQ-records-010). The fix SHALL name `chough migrate` and SHALL NOT migrate.
- `leak`: a committed record that matches REQ-records-006, with its file, line and kind and never the text. The fix SHALL name `chough doctor --fix --redact`, which SHALL redact the matches in place and is not a decision, except a match in an ID, a front matter line that gives a record's `id`, its aliases or an ID it references (REQ-records-003): `--redact` SHALL never rewrite an ID, since a redacted ID no longer reads. That finding SHALL name the recovery instead, `chough session shelve <id>` for a session record and a reviewed hand edit for any other record, and SHALL stay `skipped` under `--fix --redact`, while the other matches of the same record are redacted.
- `stale_pointer`: the active pointer names a session that is missing or archived. The fix SHALL remove the pointer.
- `finished_session`: an open session that is finished (REQ-history-006) and has no open awaiting-human gate. The fix SHALL archive it. A session with unfolded inbox entries SHALL instead be `skipped`, naming `chough inbox fold`.
- `unfinished_archive`: a session folder whose story exists and that still holds files. The fix SHALL remove every file in the folder whose bytes are committed at HEAD (REQ-records-012), except two kinds it SHALL keep, so no work is lost (#381): an evidence entry, with its log and screenshot, that the story does not hold, which is one unless one of the story's receipts has the entry's ID and the receipt the entry gives now (REQ-history-006), so an entry that differs from its receipt is kept too; and an unfolded inbox entry under `agent/inbox/`. A merge that keeps main's archive of a session to which a branch added evidence or an inbox entry leaves such an entry. The finding SHALL then be `skipped`, naming each kept entry, that the story does not hold it, and the way out by hand: keep it by moving it with `git mv`, with its log and screenshot, into an open session's `evidence/` folder (an inbox entry into that session's `agent/inbox/`, then `chough inbox fold`) and committing that, or drop it with `git rm` in a reviewed commit; then run `chough doctor --fix` again. macOS metadata in the folder (REQ-cli-068), committed or not, SHALL be removed with it and never block the fix (#377). Any other file it keeps SHALL be reported as `skipped`, naming the commit to make.
- `stale_gates`: an open session whose native change is archived on HEAD and that has open awaiting-human gates. This fix is a decision. With `--reason`, it SHALL withdraw those gates as `chough checkpoint --clear-awaiting-human` does, recording no human decision, SHALL write that reason, in the person's words and checked as REQ-records-006 checks any written value, as the `withdrawn` field of each withdrawn gate's `gate-closed` entry, so the archived story says why each gate was withdrawn, and SHALL then archive the session as the `finished_session` fix does. A decision fix that was applied SHALL echo the reason it wrote in its finding as `decision_reason`.
- `unfinished_session`: an open, unfinished session named with `--close <id>`. This fix is a decision. With `--reason`, it SHALL close the session as `chough session close <id> --reason` does.
- `timeline_rewritten`: a session or story whose Timeline at HEAD is not an exact prefix of its working Timeline (REQ-history-002). It has no fix; the finding names the entries that differ. An entry SHALL NOT count as rewritten when it equals what redaction makes of HEAD's entry, checked by redacting HEAD's text again as `chough doctor --fix --redact` does, since restoring it would restore the leak; committing the redaction is the way on (#374). Any other difference, a hand edit that writes `<redacted:<kind>>` over ordinary words included, still counts.
- `stale_bug_links`: a bug whose body links an open session's evidence entry that left with its archived session, while that session's story holds it as a receipt. The finding names each such entry and the story. The fix, which is not a decision, SHALL append one Log entry to the bug that links each entry to the archived story (REQ-cli-012) and SHALL change no earlier line; a bug whose body already links the story for an entry is not found for it.
- `alias_conflict`: an ID that is the `id` or alias of two records (REQ-records-003). It has no fix; the finding names both records.
- `oversize`: a committed record, evidence entry or screenshot over its bound (REQ-records-001, REQ-records-005, REQ-records-013). It has no fix; the finding names the file, its size and the bound, and the verify lane's `leaks` step fails on it as it does on `leak`, so CI refuses an oversized receipt.
- `unreadable`: a committed Chough record under `.chough/` that its reader refuses, such as front matter outside the subset of REQ-records-001, which makes `chough status` and `chough next` refuse the project. It has no fix; the finding names the file and the reader's reason with its line, and names `chough session shelve <id>` for an open session's `session.md`, except that a record whose front matter holds a Git conflict marker SHALL name resolving the conflict instead, as its `conflict_markers` finding does (REQ-records-001, #374). A record over its bound is `oversize` instead.
- `records_in_verification`: `.specsync/sdd.json` exists and its `verification_ignored_paths` lacks one of Chough's record folders (REQ-records-014). It has no fix, because Chough never writes SpecSync's policy; the finding names the entries to add.
- `merge_drivers`: in a Git work tree, a clone whose Git config has no command for `merge.chough-bug.driver` or `merge.chough-generated.driver` (a fresh clone has neither), or whose `.gitattributes` lacks one of the merge lines of REQ-cli-047. It is one finding, naming the missing drivers and lines. The fix SHALL set the missing drivers in the clone's local Git config and SHALL NOT write `.gitattributes`, a committed file a person reviews. A missing line SHALL leave the finding `skipped`, naming the lines to add and `chough init`, which appends exactly those.
- `merge_driver_outdated`: the merge drivers are set, and the `chough` they run, found through `PATH` as Git finds it, is missing or reports a version older than the running Chough, so a merge would run older code on these records. It has no fix; the finding names both versions and the install command from the README's toolchain section.

The fixes SHALL run in that order. They SHALL never replace a file, and SHALL leave the bytes of every session and story they do not fix unchanged. Trace: DRIVE-17, DRIVE-16, DRIVE-13, DRIVE-3, DRIVE-4, LIFE-26, LIFE-20.

Acceptance Criteria
- A finished session at stage intent and one at done are archived, a stale pointer is removed, and open work stays open.
- An unmigrated tree reports `unmigrated` naming `chough migrate`, and `--fix` changes nothing under `.chough/`.
- A committed home path reports `leak` without the path; `--fix --redact` replaces it with `<redacted:home-path>` and the next run finds nothing.
- A finished session folder holding an uncommitted file is `unfinished_archive`; `--fix` keeps that file, and after a commit removes the folder.
- A hand-edited earlier timeline entry reports `timeline_rewritten`, and an alias held by two bugs reports `alias_conflict`, each with no fix applied.
- Without `--reason`, a stale gate and a session named by `--close` are skipped with their files unchanged; with `--reason` the gate is withdrawn with no human decision recorded and the session is archived, and the named session is closed with the reason.
- A committed evidence entry whose front matter holds a bare `- -c` block item reports `unreadable` naming the file and the line, and `--fix` leaves it byte-identical and the finding `skipped`.
- A clone with neither merge driver set reports one `merge_drivers` finding naming `chough doctor --fix`, and `--fix` sets both. A `.gitattributes` missing a merge line is reported naming that line, and `--fix` leaves `.gitattributes` byte-identical and the finding `skipped`.
- With both merge drivers set and a `chough` first on `PATH` that reports an older version, `chough doctor` reports one `merge_driver_outdated` finding naming both versions and exits 1; with the same version, or with no driver set, it reports none.
- A committed session record whose `cases` reference holds a token, and whose Goal holds an email, gives a `leak` finding for each line; the one for the ID names `chough session shelve <id>`. `--fix --redact` redacts the email, leaves the ID line byte-identical with its finding `skipped`, and the session still reads.
- After a merge that kept main's archive of a session to which a branch added an evidence entry and an inbox entry, `--fix` removes the folder's other committed files and keeps both entries; the finding is `skipped`, naming each and the way out by hand; the kept files are byte-identical, and after the way out the evidence entry resolves again (#381).
- An evidence entry whose ID a receipt holds but whose receipt it no longer matches is kept as well (#381).
- A `.DS_Store` or `._session.md` in an archived session's folder does not block `--fix`, which removes the folder (#377).
- After `--fix --redact` redacts a leak in a committed Timeline entry, the next run reports no `timeline_rewritten`; a hand edit that writes `<redacted:token>` over ordinary words of an entry is still reported (#374).
- A committed session whose front matter holds a conflict reports `conflict_markers` naming each marker and its line, and its `unreadable` finding names resolving the conflict rather than `chough session shelve` (#374).

### REQUIREMENT REQ-history-006

Sessions in their own folders. `chough session new <key>` SHALL create `.chough/sessions/<id>/session.md` by create-new, where `<id>` is the date and the slug of `<key>` (REQ-records-003) and `<key>` is kept as an alias, with its own timeline, and SHALL point at it; `session use <id>` SHALL point at an open session by ID, alias or the bare slug when exactly one open session has it; an active pointer that holds such an alias or bare slug, as a 0.5.x pointer that an in-place migration keeps, SHALL name that session, and `chough doctor` SHALL NOT report it as `stale_pointer`; `session list` SHALL report each session's ID, aliases, stage, status (`archived` for an archived story, otherwise its stage status) and whether it is active, open or archived. A session is finished when it is at stage done or its native change is archived on HEAD, at any stage, as the archive's `finalization.json` records, read without a native read (DRIVE-16). A session SHALL close only itself, at its own move into done or with `chough session close --reason`: `session new` and `session use` SHALL NOT archive, close or change any other session, even a finished one (#207, #214). `session list`, `chough next` and `chough doctor` SHALL list each finished open session with its fix, `chough doctor --fix` (REQ-history-012), which keeps one with unfolded inbox entries or an open awaiting-human gate open and names the recovery: `chough inbox fold`, or `chough checkpoint --close-gate`, since closing a gate is a decision (#181). When `chough stage advance` moves the active session into done and its save succeeds, after the deferred knowledge step of REQ-cli-043, the helper SHALL archive that session in the same command and clear the active pointer, reporting `applied.session_archive` with status `archived` and the ID; a session with unfolded inbox entries SHALL stay open with its pointer, reported as `kept_open` with the count; a session for which release is authorized at that move (a run consent or a standing preference, as `stage enter release` requires) SHALL stay open for the release, reported as `kept_open` with that reason, and SHALL archive itself the same way at the move from release into done. The step SHALL NOT undo the move into done. To archive a session, Chough SHALL write `.chough/archive/sessions/<id>.md` by create-new: the session's front matter with `stage: done` (or the stage it closed at), `stage_status: archived`, no `next`, since the session's next action no longer applies, `closed` (the UTC date) and `outcome` (`done` or `closed-without-done`), and no receipts, so the story comes first; then the story as written; then, last, a `## Receipts` section holding `receipts` as one fenced YAML block, one map per evidence entry the session holds, ordered by its observation time, oldest first, then by ID, with its `id`, `aliases` (one scalar, comma-and-space separated), `claim`, `result`, `observed` (the entry's observation time, ISO UTC, REQ-cli-017), `duration_seconds` (the run's wall-clock seconds, when known), `command`, `revision`, `environment`, `content`, `fingerprint`, `raw_log` when present, `case`, `case_digest`, `criteria` and `requirements` (each one scalar, comma-and-space separated) and `limitations` (one scalar, semicolon-and-space separated) when the entry has any, so the story says what was verified against what and its stated limits stay with it, `screenshot` when present and `actor` when known (REQ-records-007), so a bug whose evidence is archived keeps its verified state, which needs a pass observed after its latest failure (REQ-cli-012), and the timing report can order and time case runs read from receipts (REQ-reporting-001); readers SHALL read receipts from that section and, for a story written before it, from the front matter. In the story, What was verified SHALL give the latest receipt of each case and every receipt bound to no case, one line each (its claim, then, after ` - `, a plain dash and never an em dash, its command unless the claim already holds it, and the first 12 digits of its fingerprint, then `; limits: ` and its limitations when it has any, so its stated limits read beside the claim, ending `^<evidence id>`) for each entry not already listed, with one line counting the earlier runs of those cases, which stay in `receipts`; a `## Bugs` section SHALL name each bug in the session's checkpoint with its title and status, linked to its record; and the story SHALL end with the Timeline and a closing line `Closed <date>: <outcome>.`, before `## Receipts`. When the story's Goal paragraph is not the session's current objective, as after a later `checkpoint --objective` (REQ-records-004), the story SHALL get one line `Objective at closeout: <objective>` after the Goal paragraph; nothing in the title, the Goal or elsewhere SHALL be rewritten. Chough SHALL read the story back and compare it before it removes anything, and SHALL then remove the session folder, `session.md`, `evidence/` and `agent/` included, and move its screenshots beside the story (REQ-records-013), each file only under REQ-records-012; files it keeps SHALL be reported, the folder SHALL count as archived while its story exists, and `chough doctor --fix` SHALL finish the removal once they are committed, keeping the evidence and inbox entries REQ-history-012 keeps (#381). Archiving a session SHALL NOT read, move, rewrite or remove any other session's folder or story. After it, until a session is opened, `next` SHALL say there is no open session and name `chough session new <key>`, exiting 0, and `status` SHALL refuse with that message (DRIVE-13). Evidence and bug links into an archived session SHALL resolve to its story's receipts. Two branches that record work in different sessions SHALL merge without conflict. A session that cannot be read, because it is over the bound of REQ-records-001 or invalid, SHALL NOT stop `session list`, `session new`, `session use` or another session's `next`; `session list` SHALL report it with the reason and the recovery `chough session shelve <id>`, which SHALL move the folder unparsed and byte for byte to `.chough/archive/shelved/<id>/` with a `shelved.md` giving its size, SHA-256 per file, time and reason, and clear the active pointer when it named that session. Shelving SHALL never replace a file, and no reader SHALL scan `.chough/archive/shelved/` (DRIVE-14). `chough session close <id> --reason <text>` SHALL close an open session for abandoned work without a native read: it SHALL refuse with nothing changed a blank reason, a session that is archived or does not exist, and a session with unfolded inbox entries, naming `chough session use <id>` and `chough inbox fold`; otherwise it SHALL withdraw each open awaiting-human gate of that session as `chough checkpoint --clear-awaiting-human` does, one `gate-closed` entry each and no human decision, name them in its output under `withdrawn_gates`, so the story lists no gate as awaiting, add the decision `Closed without done: <reason>` and archive the session as above with outcome `closed-without-done`, clearing the active pointer when it named that session (DRIVE-16). Trace: DRIVE-3, DRIVE-24, DRIVE-25, KNOW-30, KNOW-34, LIFE-3, LIFE-7, DRIVE-14, DRIVE-13, DRIVE-16.

- A session whose objective changed after its Goal was filled archives a story with one line `Objective at closeout: <current objective>` after its unchanged Goal paragraph; a session whose Goal is its objective archives a story without that line.
