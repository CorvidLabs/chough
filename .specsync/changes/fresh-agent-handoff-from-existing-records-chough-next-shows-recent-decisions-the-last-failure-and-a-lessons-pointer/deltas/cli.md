Trace: LIFE-3, LIFE-6, LIFE-1, FLOW-12.

- Native module .3md is the lesson source where present; otherwise the module context.md is used.
- The canonical skill states the three certainty labels, that each lesson names its source and limits, and that a project decision outranks a lesson; no Chough lesson store, index or ledger exists.

### REQUIREMENT REQ-cli-021

Focused next action. `chough next` SHALL print a compact action view, separate from the full `chough status` report: the objective; the current stage with its status, iteration and side-stage callers; the recorded native change with its read state, summary state, approval and correction validity, terminal evidence validity, native next action and approved-delta result; the next valid action with its reason and, when one applies, its command; the gate for the next main stage; awaiting-human gates; blockers; open acceptance-blocking bug IDs with the count of other open bugs; only the referenced observations that are not current, with counts for the rest; the most recent checkpoint decisions, at most 5 with their total, showing the kind and reason of entries that follow the `decided:`, `rejected:` or `failed:` prefix convention and other entries whole; the latest failed observation by its recorded observation time, with the status of each bug that links it; and a pointer to each module's native lesson source with the count and bounded paths of archived SpecSync lesson bundles, without reading lesson text or tracking which bundles were folded. It SHALL print text by default and the same fields as JSON with `--json`, and each form SHALL be at most 16384 bytes, with every list bounded, its total stated and any truncation marked. The same records SHALL give byte-identical output; ordering SHALL use recorded fields and names, never file modification times. `chough next` and `chough status` SHALL derive the next valid action from the same rules and SHALL each make at most one native status read. In stage done, when the recorded native change is archived with authenticated terminal evidence or no native change is recorded, the next valid action SHALL recommend a new cycle, a stage reset that carries a reason followed by a new native change, rather than repeat the checkpoint's next action; a stage reset SHALL replace the checkpoint's next action. In an earlier main stage, when the recorded native change is archived with authenticated terminal evidence and no pause, human gate or blocking bug applies, the next valid action SHALL NOT continue the stage; it SHALL say the change is archived and recommend the new cycle, after recording done first at accept. Trace: LIFE-3, FLOW-13, FLOW-2, LIFE-7, LIFE-1, FLOW-12.

Acceptance Criteria
- With 500 referenced stale observations, 40 awaiting-human gates and 40 blockers of 4 KiB each, 50 decisions of 4 KiB each, 100 failed observations, 40 bugs linking the latest failure, 40 archived lesson bundles, 16 modules in the checkpoint and a native response near the parse bound, the text and JSON forms each stay within 16384 bytes, list at most 10 entries per list and state each total.
- The view lists referenced observations that are historical, pruned or unavailable and counts referenced current and unreferenced ones; it carries no raw native output, no observation record and no lesson text.
- The view shows the last 5 decisions newest first with the kind and reason of prefixed entries and unprefixed entries whole; the failed observation with the greatest recorded time, ties broken by the greater observation ID, with each linking bug's status; and each module's lesson source path, at most 10 with their total, with the archived bundle count and up to 5 paths.
- Two runs over the same records, and a run after file modification times change, give byte-identical text and JSON.
- `chough next` and `chough status` report the same next valid action for the same records, and each runs `specsync change status` at most once.
- In stage done with an archived, authenticated change, the next valid action recommends `chough stage reset` with `--reason` and `specsync change new`, and the suggested reset succeeds; after a reset the checkpoint's next action is the new-cycle text, and back leaves it unchanged.
- At intent, design, plan, build or test with an archived, authenticated change and no pause, human gate or (at test) blocking bug, the next valid action recommends the same new cycle and not `Continue <stage>`; at accept it names `chough stage advance` to done first.
