## MODIFIED

### REQUIREMENT REQ-cli-008

Stage routing bounded by native gates. The helper SHALL route the stages intent, design, plan, build, test, accept and done, the optional release stage, and the side stages bugs, validate and librarian. It SHALL record each advance, iterate, back, reset, pause, resume, skip and side-stage enter or return as an append-only transition with its reason. Back and reset SHALL list the later stages whose outputs need refresh and SHALL point to native SpecSync status for any reapproval or recovery. Every move SHALL meet the prerequisite of its destination, whichever command makes it: build SHALL require the recorded native change to be approved, implementing or verifying with a valid definition approval; test and accept SHALL require the build prerequisite or the done prerequisite; done SHALL require the change to be archived with authenticated terminal evidence. Unavailable, unknown or undecodable native state, an approval reported invalid, unauthenticated archive evidence and an invalid SDD policy SHALL refuse those moves; a valid disabled policy or no policy SHALL leave them ungated. The helper SHALL NOT record or infer human approval or review. Leaving test for accept, by advance or skip, SHALL also be refused with `refused:unlinked-failure` while the latest observation, by recorded time and then ID, of a case referenced in the checkpoint failed and no local bug links that observation; the refusal SHALL name the case and the observation and SHALL say to rerun the case or link the observation to a bug. A later passed observation of that case, or any local bug linking the failed observation, SHALL lift it; stale observations that did not fail and cases not referenced in the checkpoint SHALL NOT trigger it. At test, `chough next` and `chough status` SHALL name the same refusal from the same rule. Trace: FLOW-2, FLOW-5, FLOW-10, LIFE-1, FLOW-7, LIFE-5.

Acceptance Criteria
- Every transition kind is exercised in an isolated fixture; history survives back and reset; skip without a reason is refused.
- Entering build is refused for draft, unavailable and approval-invalid states and allowed for a valid approved state.
- Reaching test or accept is refused for draft, unavailable and approval-invalid states and allowed for a valid approved state or an archive with authenticated terminal evidence.
- Leaving accept, reset to done and release to done are refused unless the change is archived with authenticated terminal evidence.
- A malformed or ill-typed SDD policy refuses gated moves; a valid disabled policy leaves them ungated.
- Entering a side stage records its caller, and return restores that caller.
- Leaving test with a referenced case whose latest observation failed and that no bug links is refused with `refused:unlinked-failure` naming the case, the observation and both remedies, for advance and skip; a later pass or a linking bug of any status allows it; a stale passed observation or an unreferenced failed case does not refuse; at test, next and status name the same refusal.
