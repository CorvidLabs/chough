## MODIFIED

### REQUIREMENT REQ-cli-030

Open human gates hold the stage they name. Each awaiting-human gate SHALL name the stage it holds and the native step it drives: `chough checkpoint --awaiting-human "<gate>" [--holds <stage>] [--drives approve|review|none]` SHALL record `holds`, a main stage, release or a side stage, defaulting to the current stage, and `drives`, defaulting to `approve` for a gate held at plan, `review` for one held at accept and `none` otherwise (REQ-records-008). While an open gate holds the stage a move would leave, `chough stage advance` and `chough stage skip` SHALL be refused with `refused:awaiting-human` before any native read; for `stage advance --to`, every stage the move would leave counts. The refusal SHALL name at most 3 such gates, in recorded order, with the count of the rest, and SHALL say to record the human's decision on a gate with `chough checkpoint --close-gate "<gate>" --decided-by "<who>" --words "<their words>" --where "<where>" --recorded-by "<agent>"` (REQ-cli-050), and to withdraw a gate no human decision closes with `chough checkpoint --clear-awaiting-human`, re-passing gates still waiting with `--awaiting-human`; a gate that `chough doctor --fix --reason` withdraws keeps that reason on its `gate-closed` entry (REQ-history-012). No flag or reason SHALL bypass it. A gate that holds a later stage SHALL NOT refuse a move out of an earlier one, so a pending release or publication decision does not stop build, test or accept (#237). Iterate, back, reset, pause, resume and side-stage enter and return SHALL NOT be refused by any gate. Once no open gate holds the stage being left, the move SHALL be applied and gated as before. `chough next`, `chough status` and `chough gate list` SHALL list every open gate with the stage it holds; while a gate holds the current stage, the waiting next action SHALL say not to decide it for the human, SHALL name `chough checkpoint --close-gate` for recording their decision, and SHALL say that advance and skip are refused until it is closed. Trace: FLOW-3, FLOW-5, FLOW-13, FLOW-2, FLOW-16, FLOW-23.

Acceptance Criteria
- With one open gate holding the current stage, advance and skip are refused with `refused:awaiting-human` at intent, design, plan with SDD disabled, and release; the stage and the timeline are unchanged and the message names the gate, the `--close-gate` command and the clearing command.
- With 5 open gates holding the current stage the message names the first 3 and says 2 more.
- Regression for issue 237: with a gate opened at build that holds release, the advances from build to test and from test to accept apply as usual, the gate stays open and `next` lists it, and the move out of release is refused until it is closed; `stage advance --to accept` from plan is refused while a gate holds build.
- A gate opened without `--holds` holds the current stage, and a gate migrated from 0.5.x holds the stage its session was at.
- After `chough checkpoint --clear-awaiting-human`, or after `--close-gate` closes the only gate holding the stage, the same advance is applied with its usual gate label; back, iterate, pause and resume, and side-stage enter and return, still work with a gate open.
- `chough next` and `chough status` give the same waiting action, which names `--close-gate` and says advance and skip are refused until the gate is closed.
