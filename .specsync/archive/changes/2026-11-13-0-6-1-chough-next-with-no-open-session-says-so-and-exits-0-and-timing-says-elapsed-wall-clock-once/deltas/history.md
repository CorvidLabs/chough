## MODIFIED

### REQUIREMENT REQ-history-006

Sessions in their own folders. `chough session new <key>` SHALL create `.chough/sessions/<id>/session.md` by create-new, where `<id>` is the date and the slug of `<key>` (REQ-records-003) and `<key>` is kept as an alias, with its own timeline, and SHALL point at it; `session use <id>` SHALL point at an open session by ID, alias or the bare slug when exactly one open session has it; an active pointer that holds such an alias or bare slug, as a 0.5.x pointer that an in-place migration keeps, SHALL name that session, and `chough doctor` SHALL NOT report it as `stale_pointer`; `session list` SHALL report each session's ID, aliases, stage, status (`archived` for an archived story, otherwise its stage status) and whether it is active, open or archived. A session is finished when it is at stage done or its native change is archived on HEAD, at any stage, as the archive's `finalization.json` records, read without a native read (DRIVE-16). A session SHALL close only itself, at its own move into done or with `chough session close --reason`: `session new` and `session use` SHALL NOT archive, close or change any other session, even a finished one (#207, #214). `session list`, `chough next` and `chough doctor` SHALL list each finished open session with its fix, `chough doctor --fix` (REQ-history-012), which keeps one with unfolded inbox entries or an open awaiting-human gate open and names the recovery: `chough inbox fold`, or `chough checkpoint --close-gate`, since closing a gate is a decision (#181). When `chough stage advance` moves the active session into done and its save succeeds, after the knowledge refresh of REQ-cli-043, the helper SHALL archive that session in the same command and clear the active pointer, reporting `applied.session_archive` with status `archived` and the ID; a session with unfolded inbox entries SHALL stay open with its pointer, reported as `kept_open` with the count; a session for which release is authorized at that move (a run consent or a standing preference, as `stage enter release` requires) SHALL stay open for the release, reported as `kept_open` with that reason, and SHALL archive itself the same way at the move from release into done. The step SHALL NOT undo the move into done. To archive a session, Chough SHALL write `.chough/archive/sessions/<id>.md` by create-new: the session's front matter with `stage: done` (or the stage it closed at), `stage_status: archived`, no `next`, since the session's next action no longer applies, `closed` (the UTC date) and `outcome` (`done` or `closed-without-done`), and no receipts, so the story comes first; then the story as written; then, last, a `## Receipts` section holding `receipts` as one fenced YAML block, one map per evidence entry the session holds, ordered by its observation time, oldest first, then by ID, with its `id`, `aliases` (one scalar, comma-and-space separated), `claim`, `result`, `observed` (the entry's observation time, ISO UTC, REQ-cli-017), `duration_seconds` (the run's wall-clock seconds, when known), `command`, `revision`, `environment`, `content`, `fingerprint`, `raw_log` when present, `case`, `case_digest`, `screenshot` when present and `actor` when known (REQ-records-007), so a bug whose evidence is archived keeps its verified state, which needs a pass observed after its latest failure (REQ-cli-012), and the timing report can order and time case runs read from receipts (REQ-reporting-001); readers SHALL read receipts from that section and, for a story written before it, from the front matter. In the story, What was verified SHALL give the latest receipt of each case and every receipt bound to no case, one line each (its claim, its command unless the claim already holds it, and the first 12 digits of its fingerprint, ending `^<evidence id>`) for each entry not already listed, with one line counting the earlier runs of those cases, which stay in `receipts`; a `## Bugs` section SHALL name each bug in the session's checkpoint with its title and status, linked to its record; and the story SHALL end with the Timeline and a closing line `Closed <date>: <outcome>.`, before `## Receipts`. Chough SHALL read the story back and compare it before it removes anything, and SHALL then remove the session folder, `session.md`, `evidence/` and `agent/` included, and move its screenshots beside the story (REQ-records-013), each file only under REQ-records-012; files it keeps SHALL be reported, the folder SHALL count as archived while its story exists, and `chough doctor --fix` SHALL finish the removal once they are committed. Archiving a session SHALL NOT read, move, rewrite or remove any other session's folder or story. After it, until a session is opened, `next` SHALL say there is no open session and name `chough session new <key>`, exiting 0, and `status` SHALL refuse with that message (DRIVE-13). Evidence and bug links into an archived session SHALL resolve to its story's receipts. Two branches that record work in different sessions SHALL merge without conflict. A session that cannot be read, because it is over the bound of REQ-records-001 or invalid, SHALL NOT stop `session list`, `session new`, `session use` or another session's `next`; `session list` SHALL report it with the reason and the recovery `chough session shelve <id>`, which SHALL move the folder unparsed and byte for byte to `.chough/archive/shelved/<id>/` with a `shelved.md` giving its size, SHA-256 per file, time and reason, and clear the active pointer when it named that session. Shelving SHALL never replace a file, and no reader SHALL scan `.chough/archive/shelved/` (DRIVE-14). `chough session close <id> --reason <text>` SHALL close an open session for abandoned work without a native read: it SHALL refuse with nothing changed a blank reason, a session that is archived or does not exist, and a session with unfolded inbox entries, naming `chough session use <id>` and `chough inbox fold`; otherwise it SHALL withdraw each open awaiting-human gate of that session as `chough checkpoint --clear-awaiting-human` does, one `gate-closed` entry each and no human decision, name them in its output under `withdrawn_gates`, so the story lists no gate as awaiting, add the decision `Closed without done: <reason>` and archive the session as above with outcome `closed-without-done`, clearing the active pointer when it named that session (DRIVE-16). Trace: DRIVE-3, DRIVE-24, DRIVE-25, KNOW-30, KNOW-34, LIFE-3, LIFE-7, DRIVE-14, DRIVE-13, DRIVE-16.

Acceptance Criteria
- Two branches that each advance a different session merge in Git with no conflict and keep both sessions' timelines.
- The move into done of the active session writes `.chough/archive/sessions/<id>.md` with every receipt and the full timeline, removes the committed session folder, clears the pointer, reports `archived`, and `next` then exits 0 naming `chough session new <key>` while `status` refuses.
- Each receipt in the story gives its entry's `observed` time, its `duration_seconds`, `raw_log` and `actor` when the entry has them, and its `aliases` as one comma-and-space separated scalar; a bug whose linked pass was observed after its latest linked failure stays verified once that session is archived.
- Regression for issue 165: with seven earlier archived stories present, among them 0.5.x-era stories, and `knowledge/` neither tracked nor ignored, advancing accept to done leaves every earlier story byte-identical in place, `git status` shows no deleted file, and the only new committed record is the new story; the same path in the 0.5.x layout is recorded failing first on the 0.5.0 code and linked to a local bug.
- With an uncommitted file in `agent/`, the move into done writes the story, keeps that file and its folder, reports it, and `next` does not treat the folder as open; after a commit, `doctor --fix` removes the folder.
- At the move into done, a session with unfolded inbox entries stays open (`kept_open` with the count).
- Regression for #207: with a finished session whose native change is archived and that still waits on a publish gate, `session new` and `session use` leave it byte-identical and open, and `next` and `doctor` list it as finished with its fix.
- With release authorized before the move into done, the session stays open (`kept_open`), `stage enter release` works, and the move from release into done archives it.
- A session whose native change is archived on HEAD, at a stage short of done, is listed as finished with no native read, and only `chough doctor --fix` archives it.
- `session list` reports open, active and archived sessions; `session use` accepts the ID, the alias and a unique bare slug.
- With one session over its bound and another unreadable, `session list` reports each with `chough session shelve <id>`, and `next` on the active session and `session new` still work; shelving moves the folder byte for byte under `.chough/archive/shelved/`.
- `chough session close <id> --reason <text>` archives the session with outcome `closed-without-done` and the reason in its decisions; a blank reason, an archived or missing session and a session with unfolded inbox entries refuse with nothing changed.
- After an in-place migration whose machine-local pointer holds a 0.5.x session key, `next` and `status` read that session, and `chough doctor` reports no `stale_pointer`.
- A story's receipts, and its What was verified lines for them, are in observation-time order, oldest first, whatever order the session referenced them in.
- A session with three runs of one case and one manual entry archives a story whose What was verified lists the case's latest run and the manual entry, each command once, and a line counting 2 earlier runs, while `receipts` keeps all four.
- An archived story's front matter gives `stage_status: archived` and no `next`.
- `chough session close <id> --reason <text>` on a session with an open human gate writes one `gate-closed` entry for it with no decision, names it under `withdrawn_gates`, and the story lists no gate as awaiting.
- An archived story starts its body within the first 20 lines of the file, ends with `## Receipts` holding every receipt, and names each bug of its checkpoint under `## Bugs`; a bug verified by a receipt in it stays verified, and a story written with receipts in its front matter still reads.
- After the move into done, `chough next` exits 0 naming `chough session new <key>`, and `chough status` refuses with the same message.
