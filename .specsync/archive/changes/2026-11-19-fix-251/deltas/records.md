### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-014

Chough's records stay out of SpecSync's verification. The folders Chough writes while work is in progress, `.chough/sessions/`, `.chough/archive/` and `.chough/bugs/`, SHALL hold only Chough's records: no case definition, preference, reading guide or other input that a change's verification needs SHALL be written under them, and `.chough/cases/`, `.chough/preferences.json`, `.chough/README.md` and `knowledge/` SHALL stay outside them. These three prefixes are what a project lists in SpecSync's documented exclusion, `verification_ignored_paths` in `.specsync/sdd.json` (SpecSync 6.1.2 and later), so that a Chough record written after `specsync change check --commit`, whether left untracked or committed, leaves the recorded verification current, in the author's tree and in a reviewer's clean checkout alike; `.chough/local/` is already Git-ignored and never part of it. Chough SHALL read that policy and SHALL never write it: `chough doctor` SHALL report `records_in_verification` (REQ-history-012) while `.specsync/sdd.json` exists and its list lacks any of the three prefixes, naming the missing entries, the SpecSync version that supports them and that adding them is an ordinary reviewed change to `.specsync/sdd.json`; `chough init` SHALL print the same advice; and the migration plan and receipt SHALL list it as a follow-up (REQ-records-009). `chough next` SHALL say when Chough writes are safe: with the three entries in force, that Chough's records may be written and committed at any point of the native order; without them, that Chough's records are committed before `change check --commit` and nothing under `.chough/` is written until finalize. While SpecSync covers the whole repository, through `source_dirs` holding `.` in `.specsync/config.toml` or `.` in the policy's `meaningful_paths`, SpecSync refuses those entries as overlapping, so the advice of `chough doctor` and `chough init` SHALL instead name those settings, say why (SpecSync refuses an entry that overlaps them and, for `source_dirs`, counts the skill bundle `chough init` installs as uncovered source), and say to narrow `source_dirs` to the folders that hold the code, for example `["src"]`, and keep `.` out of `meaningful_paths`, then list the missing entries; When a file a spec covers sits at the repository root, so there is no code folder to narrow to (#354), the advice SHALL instead say to add the skill bundle's and Chough's folders (`.chough`, and `.agents` or the host's skill folder) to `exclude_dirs`, which keeps them out of coverage, and that the list needs the code moved into a folder or SpecSync's fix, CorvidLabs/spec-sync#1083. `chough init` SHALL give its advice with no policy too, and Chough SHALL write neither file. When SpecSync refuses the list because an open change's affected paths overlap it, Chough SHALL pass that refusal through and name the change. Trace: DRIVE-4, FLOW-16.

Acceptance Criteria
- With the three entries listed and real SpecSync at the CI pin, an untracked evidence entry present while `change check --commit` runs leaves verification current in that tree and in a clean clone of the pushed commit, whose workspace digest matches the recorded one (spec-sync #1045's case); the same steps without the entries leave the clone's verification stale, as the control.
- With the three entries listed, a `chough checkpoint`, a `chough evidence add` and a `chough bug add` written after `change check --commit`, and then committed, leave `specsync change status` reporting verification current and the audit passing, and the review then records (the #212 case); without the entries the same writes make verification stale, as the control.
- A case definition changed under `.chough/cases/` after `change check --commit` still makes verification stale, so nothing a change's verification needs is hidden.
- With a prefix missing, `chough doctor` reports `records_in_verification` naming the missing entries and writes nothing; with all three listed it reports nothing; `.specsync/sdd.json` is byte-identical after every Chough command.
- `chough next` says Chough's writes are safe at any point with the entries in force, and gives the commit-first order without them.
- With `source_dirs = ["."]` and no policy, `chough init` prints the narrowing advice naming it, the overlap refusal and the skill bundle; with a policy whose `meaningful_paths` also holds `.`, `chough doctor`'s `records_in_verification` names both settings and says to narrow, then list the missing entries; once both are narrowed the advice is the list again; `.specsync/config.toml` and `.specsync/sdd.json` stay byte-identical.
- With `source_dirs = ["."]` and a spec covering a file at the repository root, `chough init` and `chough doctor` advise adding `.chough` and the installed skill folder to `exclude_dirs` and say the list needs the code in a folder or CorvidLabs/spec-sync#1083, never naming a folder to narrow to; once those folders are excluded, only the list's part is left.
