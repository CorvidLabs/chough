## MODIFIED

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-011

Journal and feed. The knowledge vault's journal SHALL be dated Markdown posts in `knowledge/journal/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>.md`, each with front matter giving `id`, `title`, `type: journal`, `tags`, `date`, `time` (the UTC time it was written, ISO 8601 to the second; the file name keeps only the date), `kind` (`shipped`, `closed`, `intent`, `spec`, `broke` or `learned`), `links` and, for a finished session, `record`, the path of its archived story. `chough checkpoint --title <title>` SHALL record the session's post title, one line of at most 72 bytes, as `title`, and `chough checkpoint --summary <text>` its post summary of at most 900 bytes as `summary`, one list item per non-blank line of the text; each SHALL replace the earlier value, an empty value SHALL remove it, both SHALL pass REQ-records-006, and the story SHALL keep both, so the agent who did the work writes the post's short human title and its summary before the move into done. When a session archives itself at its move into done (REQ-history-006), Chough SHALL write a `shipped` post, or a `closed` post for a session closed without done, by create-new. Its title SHALL be the session's `title`, or, when it has none, a title derived from its objective: the text before the first `: `, `; `, `, `, ` (` or ` — ` that starts at least 16 bytes in, cut at a word to at most 72 bytes and ending in `…` when cut. Its body SHALL be the session's `summary`, one paragraph per line, or, when it has none, a body derived from the story: the Goal's first paragraph, then `Why:` with the first Decisions and why entry, then `Verified:` with the claim of the newest What was verified entry, the text before its join, the ` - ` (or, in a story archived before Chough joined with a dash, the ` — `) that is followed by the entry's backticked command or its 12 fingerprint digits, so a ` - ` inside the claim stays in it and a post copies no evidence ID, then `Next:` with the first Open threads entry, an empty part left out. The body SHALL be cut at a sentence end so that it stays within 1,200 bytes with the line that follows it, a line linking the PR when the session names one, the archived story and, when the session's native change is archived, its `.specsync/archive/changes/` folder, so the post is short and the story holds the full record. `chough journal add --record <story> --title <title> --body <text> [--link <link>]...` SHALL write the post of an archived story that has none, for a backfill of stories archived before the journal began or before this rule: its `kind` SHALL be `shipped` when the story's `outcome` is `done` and `closed` otherwise, its `date` the story's `closed` date, its `time` the latest time among the story's Timeline entries when that time falls on that date (otherwise it records none), its `record` the story, and its links those of the post at the move into done followed by each `--link`; it SHALL refuse, writing nothing, when the story does not exist or when a post's `record` already names it, naming that post. The journal and its feed SHALL be the timeline of archived changes: `knowledge/index.md` SHALL present them as that timeline and SHALL link `.specsync/archive/changes/` for changes archived before the journal began (REQ-cli-043), and no other timeline page SHALL be generated. `chough journal add --kind <kind> --title <title> --body <text> [--link <link>]...` SHALL write any other post by create-new, refusing a body over 1,200 bytes. Chough SHALL never rewrite or remove a post, so a person's edit is kept. `knowledge/journal/feed.xml` SHALL be an Atom feed of the newest 20 posts, newest first by `time`. A post without a `time`, such as a migrated or hand-written one, SHALL sort by its date and then its name, after the timed posts of the same date, and the index SHALL mark it "(no time)". The index's newest 10 posts SHALL use the same order. An entry's `<updated>` SHALL be its `time` when it has one, and the feed's `<updated>` the first entry's. The feed SHALL be generated from the posts with identical bytes for the same posts, and `chough library index` SHALL regenerate it, with `--check` covering it (REQ-cli-047). The journal SHALL copy no record: it links them. Trace: KNOW-33, KNOW-30, KNOW-31, KNOW-15, KNOW-4, KNOW-14.

Acceptance Criteria
- The move into done of a session with a PR and an archived native change writes one `shipped` post whose body is at most 1,200 bytes and links the PR, `.chough/archive/sessions/<id>.md` and the change's archive folder, and `feed.xml` lists it.
- `knowledge/index.md` names the journal and feed as the timeline of archived changes and links `.specsync/archive/changes/`, and no `timeline.md` or `timeline/` is generated.
- A person's edit to a post survives a later move into done and `library index`, and a post name that already exists is never overwritten.
- `chough journal add` writes a post of each kind, and a body over 1,200 bytes is refused with nothing written.
- Regenerating `feed.xml` twice gives identical bytes, it parses as XML, and `library index --check` fails naming it after a hand edit.
- Each new post records `time`. Posts written on one day are listed newest first in the index and the feed, a post without a `time` follows that day's timed posts and is marked "(no time)" in the index, and an entry's `<updated>` is its `time`.
- With `chough checkpoint --title` and `--summary` set, the move into done writes a post whose title is that title and whose body holds each summary line; without them, the title is the objective's first clause within 72 bytes and the body holds the Goal, the first decision, the newest verified entry and the first open thread, within 1,200 bytes with its links.
- `chough checkpoint --title` over 72 bytes or holding a line break, and `--summary` over 900 bytes, are refused with nothing written; an empty value removes the field.
- `chough journal add --record <story>` writes one post dated by the story's `closed`, with the story's latest Timeline time on that date, its `record` and links, of kind `shipped` for a done story and `closed` otherwise; a second `--record` for the same story, or one naming no archived story, is refused with nothing written.
- A post derived from a story whose newest What was verified line uses the earlier ` — ` join gives that line's claim alone, as it does for a line joined with ` - `, and a claim that itself holds ` - ` is kept whole with either join (#278).
