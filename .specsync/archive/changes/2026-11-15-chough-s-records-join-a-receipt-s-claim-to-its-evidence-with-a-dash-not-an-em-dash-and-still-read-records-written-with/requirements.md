# Requirements

REQ-history-006: a story's What was verified line joins its claim to its command and fingerprint digits with ` - `, never an em dash. REQ-records-011: a post's `Verified:` line gives the claim before ` - `, or before ` — ` in a story archived earlier, so it copies no evidence ID. REQ-cli-061: a gate summary's `Change:` line joins the change's ID and title with ` - `. Each gains one acceptance criterion. See `deltas/history.md`, `deltas/records.md` and `deltas/cli.md`.
