## MODIFIED

### REQUIREMENT REQ-cli-017

Evidence records. The helper SHALL record each observation as an evidence entry of the active session, `.chough/sessions/<session>/evidence/<id>.md` (REQ-records-001, REQ-records-003), whose front matter gives `kind: evidence`, `id`, `aliases`, `claim` (the summary), `result` (`passed`, `failed` or `not-run`), `evidence_kind`, `method`, `track`, `case` and `case_digest` when it is bound to a case, `command` (the executed argument vector), `exit_code`, `tests_run`, `revision`, `content` (the working content identity, a SHA-256 digest), `environment`, `fingerprint` and `raw_log` (REQ-records-005), `external` or `screenshot` when present (REQ-records-005, REQ-records-013), `criteria`, `requirements`, `contracts`, `limitations` (the receipt that an archived session's story keeps of the entry holds its `criteria`, `requirements` and `limitations` too, REQ-history-006), `observed` (the UTC time), `duration_seconds` (the run's wall-clock seconds, when known, which the timing report reads for case run times, REQ-reporting-001) and `actor` (REQ-records-007), and whose body is the claim as one bold sentence and one plain sentence saying what the receipt shows, followed, when it failed, by why when Chough knows it, such as an expected output that was missing, with the expected text, and then the key failing lines. The kinds product-test, structural, human-observation, delivery-observation, publication-observation, installation-observation, deployment-observation, live-health-observation and dry-run SHALL be accepted. Freshness (REQ-cli-004) SHALL read an entry, and a receipt in an archived story or bug, the same way; a receipt migrated from an observation with no content identity SHALL keep the revision-bound freshness result. A successful `evidence add` response SHALL retain every existing key and SHALL add `observation`, containing the stored evidence ID; that ID SHALL equal both the file stem in `recorded` and the stored record's `id`. Trace: LIFE-2, LIFE-7, FLOW-7, DRIVE-25, DRIVE-26, LIFE-19.

Acceptance Criteria
- `evidence add` and `cases run` each write one entry with every listed key that applies and the one-line claim, and no JSON file; a `cases run` entry gives its run's `duration_seconds`.
- Every listed kind is accepted, and a dry run is reported as a dry run, not as a delivery kind.
- An entry whose content identity matches the working tree reads current and a change to observed content makes it stale; a receipt in an archived story reads the same; a migrated receipt without a content identity keeps its revision-bound result.
- A successful `evidence add` response returns `observation` beside every pre-existing key; `observation` equals the `recorded` path's file stem and the stored record's `id`.
- A failed case run whose expected output was missing has a body that says the expected output was missing and quotes it, before its key failing lines.
