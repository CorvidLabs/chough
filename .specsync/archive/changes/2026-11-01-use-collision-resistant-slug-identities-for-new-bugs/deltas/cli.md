## MODIFIED

### REQUIREMENT REQ-cli-012

Local bug backlog. The helper SHALL persist bug records in `.chough/bugs/` with symptom, trigger, expected and actual behavior, criterion, requirement and case links, environment, revision, evidence references, impact, classification, scope and status. It SHALL list open bugs for a fresh session and SHALL search existing records by symptom, trigger and affected IDs before a new record is added. A bug SHALL remain unverified until a failed observation is linked, and a regression classification SHALL require a passed observation at an ancestor revision. A bug SHALL read as reproduced and later passed, and SHALL accept the verified status, only when a present linked passed observation was observed strictly after its most recent present linked failed observation. Additional evidence SHALL be appended rather than overwritten. The helper SHALL render a report candidate and SHALL NOT publish it. Every new bug SHALL use an ID in the form `BUG-<lowercase-title-slug>-<24-lowercase-hex>`, generated once by the same generator for manual `bug add` and automatic failure capture. The title slug SHALL be derived by lowercasing ASCII letters, replacing each maximal run of characters outside ASCII `a` to `z` and `0` to `9` with one hyphen, and trimming leading and trailing hyphens; if the result is empty it SHALL be `bug`. The slug SHALL be truncated to at most 71 bytes and any trailing hyphen left by truncation SHALL be removed, so the complete ID is at most 100 bytes and the final hyphen and all 24 digest digits are preserved. The digest suffix SHALL be the first 24 lowercase hexadecimal digits of SHA-256 over a canonical creation payload containing a format version, a bug-identity domain separator and all initial bug fields other than the ID and update timestamp. An update SHALL NOT recompute the ID or rename the record, even when stored creation content has changed. The bug record schema SHALL remain 1. Existing `BUG-NNNN` records SHALL remain readable and usable without migration or renumbering, and duplicate and related links SHALL resolve in either direction across legacy and new forms. New records SHALL be written by atomic create-new with no overwrite; a same-ID different-content result SHALL be refused, SHALL leave the existing record byte-identical and SHALL NOT fall back to numeric allocation. Documentation SHALL state that older Chough versions need an upgrade to use new-form IDs. Evidence, artifact, validation, snapshot, transition, case, HI, requirement and SpecSync ID schemes SHALL remain unchanged. Trace: FLOW-8, LIFE-5.

Acceptance Criteria
- A sample failure becomes a reproduced and classified bug; a second report of the same symptom is found by search and its evidence is appended.
- A regression classification without a passed observation at an ancestor revision is refused.
- An out-of-scope bug stays in the open backlog without changing the checkpoint scope.
- Rendering a report candidate prints Markdown and performs no network action.
- A pass observed before, or in the same second as, the latest linked failure leaves the bug reproduced and the verified status is refused; a bug with no linked failure cannot be marked verified.
- `--classification blocking` is refused with a hint to use `--scope`, and `--scope product-defect` with a hint to use `--classification`.
- Normalizing `  Fix   LOGIN... now!  ` produces the slug `fix-login-now`, a title with no ASCII alphanumeric character produces `bug`, and an overlong title produces an ID of at most 100 bytes with its complete final hyphen and 24 lowercase hexadecimal digest digits.
- Both manual `bug add` and automatic failure capture produce the new ID form with the normalized title slug and the first 24 lowercase hexadecimal SHA-256 digits for their canonical creation payloads; a known-answer vector fixes the payload version and domain separator.
- Changing a stored title or other content and then updating status or evidence leaves the bug ID and file name unchanged.
- TC-cli-0125 first records and links the pre-fix failure in which independent roots at the same legacy numeric high-water mark allocate the same ID, then records a later pass in which distinct manual and automatic reports in those roots receive different new-form IDs.
- A legacy `BUG-NNNN` record remains schema 1 and can be listed, searched, shown, updated and reported without renumbering or migration; duplicate and related links succeed from legacy to new and new to legacy.
- When different initial content would use an existing ID, atomic create-new refuses it, leaves the existing file byte-identical and creates no numeric fallback or replacement record.
- The CLI documentation tells users of older Chough versions to upgrade for new-form IDs, and evidence, artifact, validation, snapshot, transition, case, HI, requirement and SpecSync IDs keep their existing forms.
