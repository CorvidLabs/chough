## MODIFIED

### REQUIREMENT REQ-records-008

A human's gate approval drives the native approval as that human. Chough SHALL read human identities from configuration, never from its source: the user configuration file `chough/identity.toml` under `XDG_CONFIG_HOME` (by default `~/.config`), overridden by `.chough/local/identity.toml` when present, each `[[human]]` giving `name`, the display name records show, optional `aliases`, and `specsync_actor`, the actor SpecSync records. Every close of a gate with a human's decision (REQ-cli-050, REQ-cli-059, REQ-cli-061), whatever the gate drives and with or without `--no-native`, SHALL resolve `--decided-by` to one configured human by name or alias before anything runs or is written. When the gate drives a native step (REQ-cli-030), Chough SHALL then run, before it records anything, `specsync change approve <id> --actor <specsync_actor> --note <note> --words <words> --where <where> --recorded-by <agent>` for a gate that drives approval, the note giving the human's exact words, where and when they decided, and the agent that relayed or recorded it, or `specsync change review <id> --reviewer <specsync_actor> --verdict <pass or block> --words <words> --where <where> --recorded-by <agent>` for one that drives review, with the verdict given as `--verdict` on the close, which SHALL be required there and SHALL never be read from the words; `--words`, `--where` and `--recorded-by` SHALL carry the relay's words, place and recording agent, so SpecSync's approval and review record the relayed decision. A human decision that is neither an approval nor a review, such as a request for changes, SHALL be recorded with `--no-native`, which closes the gate with the relay and runs nothing; Chough SHALL NOT decide which case applies from the words. When SpecSync refuses, Chough SHALL pass its output and exit code through and record nothing; when it succeeds, Chough SHALL close the gate with the relay and add the decision, which SHALL name the native step run. `--decided-by` naming no configured human, or two, SHALL be refused as `refused:human-identity-unknown`, naming both configuration files and the keys to add, before anything runs; Chough SHALL NOT guess an identity or fall back to an agent account. A gate that drives no native step SHALL run no native step, and its decision is still in the configured human's name. An agent that approves or reviews on authority the human gave in advance, without the human answering that gate, SHALL record the native step as the acting agent, `<agent>-<terminal>` (REQ-cli-029), quoting the human's earlier words and where they said them, so only a decision made at that gate is in the human's name (FLOW-24). No source file, skill, template or document SHALL name a specific person as the default human. The relay remains an unauthenticated claim (FLOW-16). Trace: FLOW-23, FLOW-24, FLOW-16, FLOW-10, LIFE-26.

Acceptance Criteria
- With a configured human and a scope gate that drives approval, closing it with that human's relay runs `specsync change approve` once with the configured actor, a note holding the words, place and relaying agent, and the relay as `--words`, `--where` and `--recorded-by`, then closes the gate and records the decision.
- The same at an acceptance gate with `--verdict pass` runs `specsync change review --reviewer` as that human with that verdict and the relay as `--words`, `--where` and `--recorded-by`, and without `--verdict` it refuses before anything runs; `--no-native` closes a driving gate with the relay and starts no SpecSync process.
- A SpecSync refusal's output and exit code pass through, the gate stays open and nothing is written.
- An unconfigured or ambiguous `--decided-by` refuses with `refused:human-identity-unknown` naming both files, and no SpecSync process starts.
- `.chough/local/identity.toml` overrides the user file; a gate that drives nothing closes with no native call.
- An approval or review on authority given in advance names the agent account `<agent>-<terminal>` as the SpecSync actor and quotes the human's earlier words and place, and never the human as the actor.
- A search of `src/`, `skills/`, `docs/` and `README.md` finds no person's name or account given as a default identity.
- With no identity file, or one that does not name `--decided-by`, closing an intent gate that drives nothing, closing a driving gate with `--no-native`, and the same through `chough gate record` and `chough change ship --close-gate`, each refuse with `refused:human-identity-unknown` before anything runs, with every file unchanged; for a configured human each succeeds.
