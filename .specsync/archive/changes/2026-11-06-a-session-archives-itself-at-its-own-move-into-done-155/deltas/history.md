## MODIFIED

### REQUIREMENT REQ-history-006

Sessions per objective. `chough session new <key>` SHALL create `.chough/sessions/<key>.json`, a schema 2 checkpoint with its own history cycle, without replacing a file, and point at it; `session adopt <key>` SHALL move an existing checkpoint with a history cursor there byte for byte; `session use <key>` SHALL point at an open session; `session list` SHALL report each session's key, stage, status (`archived` for an archived session, otherwise its stage status) and whether it is active or archived. `session new` and `session use` SHALL move every other session at stage done to `.chough/sessions/archive/<key>.json` unchanged, except that a done session with unfolded inbox entries SHALL stay open and be reported as kept open. When `chough stage advance` moves the active session into done and its save succeeds, after the steps of REQ-cli-041, REQ-history-011, REQ-cli-043 and REQ-cli-049, the helper SHALL move that session to `.chough/sessions/archive/<key>.json` unchanged, in the same change, and clear the active pointer, reporting `applied.session_archive` with status `archived` and the key; a session with unfolded inbox entries SHALL stay open and the active pointer SHALL be kept, reported as `kept_open` with the count of unfolded entries; a session for which release is authorized at that move (a run consent or a standing preference, as `stage enter release` requires) SHALL stay open for the release, reported as `kept_open` with that reason, and SHALL archive itself the same way at the move from release into done; without sessions (the single checkpoint) it SHALL report `skipped`. The step SHALL NOT undo the move into done. After it, `next` and `status` SHALL refuse with "no open session" and name `chough session new <key>` until a session is opened, and a later `session new` or `session use` SHALL have no session of that change to archive (DRIVE-13). `chough inbox add --session <key>` SHALL create one entry file under `.chough/sessions/<key>.inbox/` and SHALL NOT edit any session; when an `observation` entry names an observation with no record in `.chough/evidence/`, `inbox add` SHALL still add it and SHALL warn on stderr, naming the ID, and `inbox list` SHALL mark that entry `recorded: false`; `inbox fold` SHALL add the active session's unfolded entries to its lists without duplicates and move each into `folded/`. Inbox entries SHALL carry no approval, review, consent, gate or stage field. Two branches that record work in different sessions SHALL merge without conflict. Evidence retention SHALL treat every open, archived and shelved session as a checkpoint reference source, reading a shelved one within the 16 MiB ledger bound, and SHALL refuse a preview or apply when any session cannot be read. A session that cannot be read, because it is over the checkpoint cap of REQ-cli-032 or invalid, SHALL NOT stop `session list`, `session new`, `session use` or another session's `next`: `session list` SHALL report it in its own entry with the reason and the recovery `chough session shelve <key>`. `chough session shelve <key>` SHALL move `.chough/sessions/<key>.json` unparsed and byte for byte to `.chough/sessions/shelved/<key>.json`, write `.chough/sessions/shelved/<key>.shelved.json` with its size, SHA-256, time and reason, and clear the active pointer when it named that session; `chough session shelve --legacy` SHALL do the same for `.chough/session.json` as `legacy`. Shelving SHALL never replace a file, and no reader other than retention SHALL scan `shelved/` (DRIVE-14). Trace: DRIVE-3, LIFE-8, LIFE-3, LIFE-7, DRIVE-14, DRIVE-13.

Acceptance Criteria
- Two branches that each advance a different session merge in Git with no conflict and keep both sessions' history.
- Switching away from a done session moves it to the archive with its bytes unchanged.
- Folding an inbox twice adds each entry once, and adding an entry never changes a session file.
- A done session with unfolded inbox entries stays open on a switch.
- An observation referenced only by an inactive or archived session is protected from `evidence prune --apply`, and an unreadable session file refuses it with nothing removed.
- `session list` reports an archived session with status `archived`.
- `inbox add --kind observation` with an ID that has no evidence record adds the entry, warns on stderr naming the ID, and `inbox list` marks it `recorded: false`; a recorded observation gets no warning and `recorded: true`.
- With one session over the cap and another unreadable, `session list` reports each with `chough session shelve <key>`, and `next` on the active session and `session new` still work.
- `chough session shelve <key>` moves an oversized session byte for byte into `shelved/`, after which `session new` and `next` work; `chough session shelve --legacy` does the same for an oversized `.chough/session.json`.
- The move into done of the active session moves it to `.chough/sessions/archive/` and clears the active pointer, reported as `archived`; `next` then refuses naming `chough session new <key>`, and a following `session new` archives nothing.
- At the move into done, a session with an unfolded inbox entry stays open with its pointer, reported as `kept_open` with the count; without sessions the step reports `skipped`.
- With release authorized before the move into done, the session stays open (`kept_open`), `stage enter release` works, and the move from release into done archives it and clears the pointer.
