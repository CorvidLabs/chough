# Chough 0.6.0: human-first records

Chough 0.6.0 ships human-first records as one change: one readable Markdown file per record, date and slug session folders, one-file archived stories, a journal and feed, one source of truth, no leaked paths, and gate approvals in the approving human's name. It was dogfooded on the Chough repo only and took in every open Chough bug.

At plan step 3 fmt, clippy with warnings denied and the full suite passed, 561 tests with 11 ignored. After the send-back to SpecSync 6.3.0, every send-back item went red then green and the test stage was green on 6.3.0.

The maintainer re-approved the definition on 6.3.0 and accepted it after a scoped review. It shipped and was archived on SpecSync 6.3.0, the repo moved to 0.6.0 records, and the story closed done on 2026-11-12. Tagging waits for a finished dogfood, zero open bugs and the maintainer's go.
