# Keep another writer's vault copies when a refused trim rolls back

When a refused trim rolls back, Chough now compares the vault copies and keeps any that another writer changed in the meantime (#110).

The case for it, that a rollback keeps changed vault copies, passed on Linux.

The maintainer accepted PR #115 on 2026-11-05 with Record and merge, and the native review was recorded as a pass. The change was archived and the story closed done that day.
