# Bound the knowledge index and move the full list to a monthly timeline

To keep the knowledge index under its size bound, INDEX.md now lists only recent archives, and the full list of archives lives on a monthly timeline (KNOW-28).

Its one case passed on Linux in a single test run: the index stays bounded and a monthly timeline lists every archive.

The fix rode on PR #112, which the maintainer chose to widen with it. The maintainer recorded the native review as a pass on 2026-11-05 with Record and merge, and the story closed done that day.
