# chough

I want a deliberately simple, portable way to work with the agent I am already
talking to, from what I actually want through tested delivery and learning. Most
of it should be skills, plain Markdown, and context conventions, with a small Rust
helper where deterministic checks help. I want HI, SpecSync, and Fledge to keep
owning the things they already do.

Captured from the human's accepted direction on 2026-10-31. The criteria below
are a normalized transcription, not a claim that the whole lifecycle is built.
Current-agent-first is the selected first version; Rune coordination is deferred.
