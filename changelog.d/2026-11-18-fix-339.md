- Follow-ups to the conflict-marker check (#339):
  - A conflict quoted inside a fenced code block, as an example or tool output, no longer counts as a marker.
  - A lone `=======`, which a half-resolved conflict may leave but a heading underline also is, is never refused. `chough doctor` warns of it instead, as an advisory `possible_conflict_separator` finding that does not change its exit status.
  - `chough doctor` reports markers in committed case definitions, and `chough cases check` names the file, the line and the marker.
  - `chough migrate` says in its preview that the apply refuses, and its refusal names the 0.5.x file to resolve.
